# Meridian Marine Co. — Operational Transition Rules v1

## Principle

Records move through controlled states rather than arbitrary status changes.

## Actor rules

- Operators may advance operational work within their assigned authority.
- Management may override or reassign where authorized.
- Clients may approve or decline decisions within their scope but do not directly alter internal operational states.
- Vendors may update assignment progress only through the permitted vendor workflow.
- Administrators manage system-level controls, not ordinary business decisions.

## Rejection rules

Reject a transition when:

- Required information is missing.
- The actor lacks authority.
- The target state is not valid from the current state.
- Required approval is absent.
- A prerequisite action has not been completed.
- The transition would bypass an essential safety or audit control.

## Reopen rules

Reopening a closed item requires a reason and should preserve the previous closed state and history.

## Emergency rule

Safety-critical events may require immediate escalation before normal record completion. The operational record must be completed and reconciled after the immediate risk is addressed.
