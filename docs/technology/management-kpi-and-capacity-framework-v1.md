# Meridian Marine Co. — Management KPI & Capacity Framework v1

## Operating KPIs

Track:

- Visits scheduled
- Visits completed
- Reports delivered on time
- Open findings
- Critical/Urgent findings
- Overdue actions
- Finding closure rate
- Vendor response performance
- Client approval turnaround

## Client KPIs

- Active clients
- Active vessels
- Retention
- Service issues
- Referral contribution
- Package mix

## Capacity KPIs

- Visits per operator
- Average visit duration
- Average travel burden
- Report preparation time
- Open workload per operator
- Vendor coordination workload

## Management rule

Capacity should be measured against promised service standards, not simply maximum theoretical visits.

## Early-stage priority

During the pilot, favor a small number of reliable metrics that reveal service quality and operational bottlenecks. Avoid building a dashboard full of vanity metrics before reliable underlying data exists.
