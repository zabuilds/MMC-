# Meridian Marine Co. — Minimum Operating Application Roles v1

## Operator

Performs visits, records observations, creates findings/actions, prepares reports, and manages assigned follow-up.

## Management

Oversees workload, exceptions, approvals, client issues, vendor issues, and financial/operational health.

## Client

Views their own vessel information, reports, findings, actions, requests, and approvals permitted by the service model.

## Vendor

Receives only the work and information required for assigned jobs and returns completion evidence/status.

## Administrator

Maintains system configuration, access, and controlled administrative functions.

## Access principle

Users should see only the information and actions necessary for their role. Vendor and client access must not expose unrelated clients, vessels, or internal management information.
