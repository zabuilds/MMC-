# Meridian Marine Co. — Management Command Center v1

## Purpose

Provide management with a single view of operational health, workload, exceptions, client service, vendor performance, and capacity.

## Command center sections

### Operations

- Visits today
- Upcoming visits
- Visits blocked or incomplete
- Reports pending
- Critical/Urgent findings
- Overdue actions

### Clients

- Active clients
- Clients needing attention
- Pending approvals
- Recent client communications
- Retention/service issues

### Vessels

- Vessels currently active
- Readiness status
- Open material findings
- Recent changes
- Upcoming service activity

### Vendors

- Active assignments
- Awaiting acknowledgment
- Delayed assignments
- Failed verification
- Backup coverage requirements

### Capacity

Track workload against available operating capacity:

- Visits scheduled
- Visits completed
- Operator workload
- Travel burden
- Open actions
- Reporting workload

## Exception-first management

The command center should prioritize exceptions rather than simply displaying activity counts.

Management should quickly answer:

**What is at risk? Who owns it? What happens next?**

## Financial indicators

The command center may surface approved management-level indicators such as recurring revenue, package mix, outstanding billing status, direct service cost trends, and contribution indicators once the financial data layer is implemented.

## Access

Management visibility is broader than operator/client/vendor views but remains subject to authorization and audit controls.
