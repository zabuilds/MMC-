# Meridian Marine Co. — First Service Workflow v1

## Purpose

Make the first paid MMC visit feel deliberate, professional, and more valuable than a routine inspection.

## Workflow

**PREPARE → ARRIVE → BASELINE → OBSERVE → REPORT → ORIENT → ACTIVATE**

### Prepare
Review the signed service arrangement, vessel profile, access details, owner preferences, previous records, and any known concerns.

### Arrive
Confirm safe access and establish the visit timestamp/location.

### Baseline
Perform the initial structured vessel assessment. Distinguish pre-existing conditions from new observations wherever reasonably possible.

### Observe
Complete the applicable package inspection standard and capture evidence of material findings.

### Report
Deliver the appropriate package report with condition status, priorities, evidence, and open actions.

### Orient
Where appropriate, explain the initial findings, recommended coordination, and the operating cadence to the owner.

### Activate
Set recurring visit dates, maintenance tracking, vendor contacts, readiness requirements, and unresolved baseline items.

## First-service success criteria

The owner should finish the first service knowing:

- MMC understands the vessel.
- The vessel's current baseline is documented.
- Important issues have been identified and prioritized.
- Recurring service is organized.
- The owner knows exactly how MMC will communicate.
- Any decisions or approvals needed from the owner are clear.
