# Meridian Marine Co. — Referral Partner Framework v1

## Strategic partner groups

### Brokers
Value: introduce owners at or shortly after vessel purchase.

### Captains and crew
Value: identify owners who need independent oversight when they are away.

### Marinas
Value: connect MMC with vessel owners and operational contacts.

### Marine service providers
Value: identify recurring coordination gaps while preserving clear vendor boundaries.

### Other trusted professionals
Potential relationships include insurance, legal, accounting, relocation, luxury hospitality, and property professionals where the relationship is appropriate and compliant.

## Partner proposition

MMC should position itself as a trusted extension of the owner's oversight network, not as a competitor to the partner's core service.

## Referral standards

- Respect partner relationships.
- Never poach or undermine an existing service relationship.
- Make referrals easy to initiate.
- Keep the owner relationship transparent.
- Protect client confidentiality.
- Track referral source and outcome internally.
- Follow up with partners professionally without excessive solicitation.

## Launch priority

First priority should be a small number of high-trust relationships rather than a large undifferentiated partner list.
