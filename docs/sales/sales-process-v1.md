# Meridian Marine Co. — Sales Process v1

## Sales sequence

**IDENTIFY → QUALIFY → DISCOVER → RECOMMEND → PROPOSE → ONBOARD → RETAIN**

### Identify
Find qualified owners and trusted referral partners.

### Qualify
Confirm vessel, owner situation, location, absence/travel pattern, service needs, and fit with MMC's operating boundaries.

### Discover
Understand the owner's biggest concerns, current maintenance/vendor setup, arrival patterns, previous problems, and desired level of involvement.

### Recommend
Recommend the package based on the owner's actual oversight need rather than automatically selling the highest tier.

### Propose
Present the service outcome, included scope, boundaries, price, first-service process, and next step.

### Onboard
Once accepted, collect required vessel/owner information and begin the onboarding workflow.

### Retain
Deliver consistently, communicate clearly, document value, and identify opportunities to expand service only when the owner's needs justify it.

## Sales principles

- Lead with the owner's problem, not a feature list.
- Do not create fear to close a sale.
- Do not promise technical expertise MMC does not possess.
- Do not discount by default.
- Do not pressure owners into Reserve when Steward is the better fit.
- Use the first service and documented results to demonstrate value.
- Referral relationships should be treated as long-term partnerships.

## Package recommendation logic

Harbour: owner primarily needs dependable baseline oversight.

Steward: owner wants recurring oversight plus maintenance/service coordination and readiness support.

Reserve: owner wants the highest standard of recurring attention, priority response, deeper coordination, and proactive readiness support.

Private: requirements exceed standard package boundaries or involve a complex operating arrangement.
