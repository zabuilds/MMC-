# Meridian Marine Co. — Proposal Structure v1

## Proposal objective

Make the owner's decision simple: understand the problem, understand the recommended service, understand the boundaries, and know the next step.

## Proposal sections

1. **Owner situation** — concise summary of the owner's vessel and oversight need.
2. **Recommended service** — Harbour, Steward, Reserve, or Private.
3. **What MMC will do** — package-specific scope and service frequency.
4. **What the owner receives** — reports, communication, coordination, readiness support, and documented outcomes.
5. **What remains separate** — third-party costs, specialist work, captaincy, repairs, cleaning, and other exclusions.
6. **Investment** — monthly package price and any separately approved add-ons.
7. **First service** — onboarding and baseline process.
8. **Communication** — agreed contact and escalation approach.
9. **Next step** — simple acceptance/onboarding action.

## Pricing presentation

Show the package price clearly without artificial discounting or countdown tactics.

Use the hero positioning of Steward without implying that every owner should purchase it.

## Proposal principle

The proposal should feel like a professional operating arrangement, not a generic service brochure.
