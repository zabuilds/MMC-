# Meridian Marine Co. — First 25 Client Launch Plan v1

## Objective

Acquire the first 25 recurring MMC clients through high-trust, relationship-led channels while learning which customer segments and services create the strongest recurring value.

## Client mix target

- 10 absentee owners
- 7 local/time-constrained owners
- 5 higher-value or more complex vessels
- 3 clients originating from strategic professional/referral relationships

These are planning targets, not mandatory quotas.

## Acquisition waves

### Wave 1 — First 5
Founder-led direct relationships and warm introductions.

Goal: validate positioning, onboarding, first-service experience, and actual workload.

### Wave 2 — Next 10
Expand through successful-client referrals, brokers, captains, marinas, and marine professionals.

Goal: establish repeatable acquisition channels.

### Wave 3 — Final 10
Scale the strongest channels while maintaining qualification standards.

Goal: build a healthy recurring client base without accepting poor-fit work simply to increase volume.

## First-client priorities

1. Strong fit over volume.
2. Reliable access to vessel.
3. Owners who value documentation and coordination.
4. Vessels where MMC can demonstrate clear preventive value.
5. Clients willing to provide operational feedback.

## Learning loop

After each early client onboarding, review:

- Acquisition source
- Package selected
- Time spent per visit
- Coordination time
- Reporting time
- Travel burden
- Vendor workload
- Owner questions
- Unplanned work
- Client satisfaction
- Gross contribution by package

Use these observations to refine pricing, scope, staffing, and package boundaries before aggressive growth.

## Launch discipline

Do not chase the first 25 clients at any cost. MMC's early client base should establish the reputation and operating standards that support the next 100.
