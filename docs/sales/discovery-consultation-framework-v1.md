# Meridian Marine Co. — Discovery Consultation Framework v1

## Purpose

Understand the owner's actual vessel-oversight problem before recommending a package.

## Discovery sequence

**VESSEL → OWNER → ABSENCE → CURRENT CARE → PAIN → READINESS → RESPONSE → FIT**

### Vessel
- What type and size of vessel is it?
- Where is it normally kept?
- How complex is the vessel's routine care?
- Who currently has access?

### Owner
- Where does the owner spend most of their time?
- How often are they physically with the vessel?
- Who currently handles issues when they are away?

### Absence
- How long is the vessel typically unattended?
- What concerns the owner most while away?

### Current care
- Who currently checks the vessel?
- Which vendors are involved?
- How are maintenance issues tracked?
- What happens when something goes wrong?

### Pain
Identify previous surprises, missed maintenance, unreliable vendors, poor communication, storm concerns, or readiness problems.

### Readiness
- How much preparation is normally required before arrival?
- Are fueling, servicing, provisioning, or other services coordinated?

### Response
- What level of communication does the owner want?
- What should trigger immediate contact?
- Who else is authorized to make decisions?

### Fit
Recommend Harbour, Steward, Reserve, or Private based on actual needs.

## Consultation rules

- Listen more than sell.
- Never manufacture urgency.
- Do not criticize competitors without evidence.
- Do not promise services outside MMC's scope.
- If Harbour genuinely fits, recommend Harbour.
- If needs exceed standard packages, recommend Private rather than quietly overloading a standard package.
