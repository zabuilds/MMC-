# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Portal Experience v1

## Purpose

Give vessel owners a calm, high-confidence view of their vessel without exposing unnecessary operational complexity.

## Client home

The client should immediately see:

- Vessel status
- Latest visit
- Next scheduled visit
- Open material findings
- Actions requiring attention
- Recent reports
- Approvals waiting
- Readiness status

## Vessel view

Show:

- Vessel identity
- Current status
- Recent visit history
- Active findings
- Open actions
- Vendor work relevant to the client
- Reports
- Owner instructions

## Findings

Each client-visible finding should explain:

**WHAT WAS OBSERVED → WHY IT MATTERS → CURRENT STATUS → WHAT HAPPENS NEXT**

Avoid unnecessary technical language or unsupported diagnosis.

## Approvals

Clients should be able to clearly understand:

- What decision is needed
- Why it is needed
- What MMC recommends
- Relevant evidence
- Any known timing consideration
- What happens after approval or decline

## Reports

Reports should be easy to scan, evidence-backed, and organized around decisions and actions rather than raw inspection notes.

## Communication

The portal should complement, not replace, urgent communication. Critical matters must use the approved immediate communication path.

## Privacy

Clients see only their own authorized information. Internal notes, restricted vendor information, security credentials, and unrelated operational data remain outside ordinary client views.
