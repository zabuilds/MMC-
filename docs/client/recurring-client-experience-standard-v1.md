# Meridian Marine Co. — Recurring Client Experience Standard v1

## Experience promise

MMC should make ownership feel more controlled, visible, and manageable without pretending to replace a captain, crew, marina, manager, or specialist contractor.

## Recurring cycle

**VISIT → OBSERVE → REPORT → ALERT → COORDINATE → FOLLOW UP → PREPARE → VISIT**

## Owner-facing experience

Clients should consistently understand:

- What MMC checked
- What was observed
- What changed
- What requires attention
- What MMC has already coordinated
- What requires their approval
- What happens next

## Service request handling

Requests should enter the same operational workflow regardless of whether they originate from the owner, a visit finding, a vendor recommendation, or a readiness requirement.

## Relationship standard

MMC communication should feel:

- Professional
- Discreet
- Calm
- Responsive
- Organized
- Evidence-based

## Escalation

Routine communication should never delay Critical/Urgent escalation. Material issues should be surfaced promptly through the appropriate channel.

## Continuity

A client should not have to repeatedly explain the same vessel information. The operational record should preserve relevant instructions, history, findings, actions, vendor activity, and preferences for authorized MMC personnel.
