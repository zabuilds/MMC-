# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Onboarding Lifecycle v1

## Lifecycle

**SIGN → COLLECT → VERIFY → BASELINE → ACTIVATE → FIRST VISIT → REPORT → RECUR**

### Sign
Confirm the service agreement, package, scope, pricing, communication preferences, and applicable authority limits.

### Collect
Collect client, vessel, marina/location, access, emergency, service, and communication information.

### Verify
Review the onboarding information for completeness and resolve missing or conflicting details before service activation.

### Baseline
Establish the vessel's initial documented condition, known issues, owner priorities, access instructions, vendor relationships, and special requirements.

### Activate
Create the active client/vessel operating record and schedule the first recurring service visit.

### First Visit
Conduct the initial visit using the applicable package checklist and identify baseline findings/actions.

### Report
Deliver the first report and clearly identify any owner decisions, urgent issues, vendor needs, or follow-up.

### Recur
Transition the vessel into the normal recurring oversight cycle.

## Activation rule

A vessel should not be treated as fully active until required onboarding information, access arrangements, emergency contacts, and service scope are sufficiently established for safe operation.

## Missing information

If critical onboarding information is unavailable, record the gap and determine whether service can safely proceed, must be limited, or should be delayed pending clarification.
