# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Notification Rules v1

## Notify immediately

Use prompt direct communication for:

- Critical or urgent findings
- Material security incidents
- Significant water intrusion or damage
- Material service interruption
- Owner approval required for time-sensitive action
- Major vendor failure affecting vessel readiness

## Notify promptly

- New material finding
- Meaningful scope/cost change
- Vendor schedule change affecting the owner
- Readiness issue
- Significant unresolved action

## Routine notification

- Standard visit report
- Completed routine action
- Normal vendor completion
- Monthly service summary

## Notification content

Every material notification should answer:

1. What happened?
2. What was observed or confirmed?
3. What is the current risk/status?
4. What has MMC already done?
5. What decision or approval is required?
6. What happens next?

## Communication discipline

Avoid unnecessary alarm, speculation, unsupported reassurance, or excessive operational detail. When information is incomplete, say so clearly.
