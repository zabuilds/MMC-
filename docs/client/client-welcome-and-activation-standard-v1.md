# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Welcome & Activation Standard v1

## Purpose

Create a calm, premium handoff from sale to active service.

## Welcome sequence

**WELCOME → CONFIRM → INTRODUCE → COLLECT → PREPARE → ACTIVATE**

### Welcome
Confirm that MMC has received the signed agreement and explain what happens next.

### Confirm
Confirm selected package, vessel(s), location, service start date, communication preference, and key expectations.

### Introduce
Provide the client's primary MMC contact and explain how routine requests, approvals, urgent matters, and emergencies are handled.

### Collect
Gather required client/vessel/access/emergency information and identify missing items.

### Prepare
Review the vessel baseline requirements and schedule the first visit.

### Activate
Move the client/vessel into active recurring service once required operational information is sufficiently complete.

## First-impression standard

The onboarding experience should feel organized, responsive, discreet, and confidence-building without promising services or authority outside the client's agreement.

## Activation handoff

Internal handoff should confirm:

- Client record complete
- Vessel record complete
- Package confirmed
- Access confirmed
- Emergency contacts confirmed
- Owner instructions recorded
- Baseline scheduled
- First recurring visit scheduled
- Outstanding onboarding gaps assigned
