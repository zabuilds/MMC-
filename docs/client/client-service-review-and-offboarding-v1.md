# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Service Review & Offboarding v1

## Service review

Recurring clients should periodically receive a concise review of:

- Service activity
- Visit consistency
- Open findings/actions
- Vendor activity
- Readiness activity
- Storm/emergency events where applicable
- Upcoming needs
- Recommended adjustments to service

## Review outcomes

- Continue current package
- Increase/decrease service level
- Add a custom requirement
- Change communication preferences
- Add or revise vendor coordination
- Schedule a formal service review

## Offboarding triggers

- Client request
- Vessel sale/transfer
- Service no longer required
- Material contract termination
- MMC decision to discontinue service

## Offboarding sequence

**NOTICE → CONFIRM → COMPLETE OPEN WORK → FINAL REPORT → HANDOVER → CLOSE ACCESS → ARCHIVE**

### Notice
Confirm termination date and applicable contractual requirements.

### Confirm
Identify final service date, open requests, outstanding invoices, vendor work, and owner instructions.

### Complete open work
Resolve or clearly hand over open actions where practical.

### Final report
Provide a final service summary and identify unresolved matters.

### Handover
Return or transfer authorized information, access items, keys, and relevant records according to the agreement.

### Close access
Remove operational access that is no longer required.

### Archive
Preserve required records and close the active client/vessel operating status.

## Continuity principle

Offboarding should be orderly and factual. MMC should not leave the owner, successor provider, or vessel without a clear understanding of outstanding operational matters that MMC is authorized to disclose.
