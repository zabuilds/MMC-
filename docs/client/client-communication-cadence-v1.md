# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Communication Cadence v1

## Purpose

Give owners predictable communication without creating unnecessary noise.

## Routine cadence

### After each visit
Provide the visit report and clearly identify findings, actions, approvals, and next steps.

### Material issue
Communicate promptly when a finding is Critical, Urgent, materially affects readiness, or requires an owner decision.

### Vendor activity
Provide updates when work is scheduled, materially changes, encounters a significant issue, or is completed.

### Monthly relationship touchpoint
For recurring clients, provide a concise service-level view where appropriate:

- Visits completed
- Open findings/actions
- Vendor activity
- Upcoming readiness needs
- Matters requiring attention

### Storm/emergency
Use the dedicated emergency communication procedure rather than waiting for the normal cadence.

## Communication principles

- Calm
- Factual
- Concise
- Proactive when material
- Clear about decisions required
- Clear about uncertainty
- No unsupported assurances
