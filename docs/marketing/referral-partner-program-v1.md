# Meridian Marine Co. — Referral Partner Program v1

## Ideal partners

- Marinas
- Yacht brokers
- Captains and crew networks
- Marine service providers
- Yacht management professionals
- Insurance and other trusted marine professionals where appropriate
- Property professionals serving relevant owners

## Partner value

Partners should understand MMC as a reliable independent oversight resource that can complement—not replace—their own role.

## Referral process

**INTRODUCE → QUALIFY → DISCOVER → PROPOSE → ONBOARD → UPDATE**

Where appropriate, keep the referring partner informed at a high level while protecting client confidentiality and respecting the owner's wishes.

## Partner standards

Referral partners should align with MMC's expectations for professionalism, discretion, client respect, and independence.

## Measurement

Track referral source, qualified opportunities, conversions, recurring revenue, and retention without allowing referral relationships to influence operational judgment or scope decisions.
