# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Acquisition System v1

## Positioning

MMC should lead with independent vessel oversight, visibility, accountability, and peace of mind for owners who are not continuously present.

## Acquisition funnel

**AWARENESS → INTEREST → QUALIFICATION → DISCOVERY → PROPOSAL → CONVERSION → ONBOARDING → REFERRAL**

## Core acquisition messages

### Protect the asset
Regular oversight identifies issues before they become larger problems.

### Know what's happening
Owners receive clear, evidence-based updates without needing to be physically present.

### One accountable point of coordination
MMC helps coordinate appropriate third parties while preserving clear boundaries around specialist work.

### Be ready when you arrive
Recurring oversight supports vessel readiness and reduces last-minute surprises.

## Lead qualification

Marketing should attract owners who value ongoing oversight rather than one-off transactional work.

## Channel priority

1. Referrals
2. Marina and marine professional relationships
3. Targeted owner outreach
4. Private networks
5. Digital presence

## Growth constraint

Acquisition activity should scale only in line with operational capacity and vendor coverage.
