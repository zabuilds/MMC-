# Meridian Marine Co. — End-to-End Field Visit Workflow v1

## Operating sequence

**PREPARE → ARRIVE → SECURE → OBSERVE → DOCUMENT → CLASSIFY → ACT → REPORT → CLOSE**

## 1. Prepare

Before leaving:

- Confirm vessel and client.
- Confirm package and visit type.
- Review previous report.
- Review open findings/actions.
- Review pending vendor work.
- Check access requirements.
- Confirm equipment readiness.
- Review weather and relevant safety conditions.

## 2. Arrive

- Confirm correct vessel/location.
- Follow marina/property access rules.
- Record arrival time.
- Conduct an initial external safety/environment check.

## 3. Secure

- Enter only through authorized access.
- Maintain control of keys/access devices.
- Follow vessel-specific instructions.
- Do not alter systems unnecessarily.

## 4. Observe

Use the package-specific checklist while reviewing safely observable conditions:

- Exterior
- Interior
- Water/bilge indicators
- Security
- Visible systems indicators
- Vessel presentation/readiness
- Weather/storm impacts
- Previously reported issues
- Active vendor work

## 5. Document

For material observations capture:

- What was observed
- Where it was observed
- Date/time where relevant
- Photo/video evidence where appropriate
- Existing vs newly observed condition where reasonably known

## 6. Classify

Assign finding severity:

- Critical
- Urgent
- Attention
- Monitor

Do not diagnose beyond MMC's competence. Record observable facts.

## 7. Act

For each actionable issue:

- Create an action.
- Identify owner approval requirements.
- Identify responsible party.
- Set due date where appropriate.
- Initiate authorized vendor coordination when required.
- Escalate Critical/Urgent conditions immediately under the applicable procedure.

## 8. Report

Prepare the visit report using the standard structure:

**SUMMARY → CONDITION → FINDINGS → ACTIONS → VENDORS → OWNER ATTENTION → CLOSEOUT**

Critical or Urgent matters should not wait for routine report delivery before being communicated through the appropriate escalation channel.

## 9. Close

- Verify the vessel is left in the intended condition.
- Restore access/security.
- Confirm evidence is attached/recorded.
- Confirm findings/actions are saved.
- Record vendor follow-up.
- Complete or queue the report.
- Schedule the next visit/follow-up where known.
- Record any incomplete portion of the visit.

## Incomplete visit rule

If access is unavailable, weather/safety prevents completion, a vessel condition prevents safe observation, or another material obstacle occurs, do not mark the visit as normally completed. Record the limitation, work performed, immediate risk, communication, and next step.

## Quality principle

The next authorized MMC operator should be able to understand what happened, what was observed, what was communicated, and what needs to happen next without relying on undocumented memory.
