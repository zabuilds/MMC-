# Meridian Marine Co. — Vessel Visit Checklist Standard v1

## Before arrival

- Review vessel record.
- Review previous report.
- Review open Critical/Urgent findings.
- Review open actions and vendor jobs.
- Review owner instructions.
- Check access status.
- Check weather and travel conditions.
- Confirm required field equipment.

## Arrival

- Confirm correct vessel/location.
- Confirm authorized access.
- Observe exterior condition before entering where practical.
- Note unusual access/security conditions.

## During visit

- Observe exterior condition.
- Observe interior condition.
- Check for visible water intrusion.
- Observe accessible bilge/general water condition.
- Observe visible electrical/power indicators.
- Observe HVAC/refrigeration indicators where applicable.
- Observe plumbing/water indicators where safely accessible.
- Check visible security/access condition.
- Check for obvious storm/weather impacts.
- Follow vessel-specific instructions.
- Capture evidence for material findings.

## Findings

Classify material observations as:

- Critical
- Urgent
- Attention
- Monitor

Create an action when follow-up is required.

## Closeout

- Confirm vessel is left in the required condition.
- Secure access.
- Record completion time.
- Update findings/actions.
- Record vendor activity observed.
- Prepare report.
- Escalate material issues.
