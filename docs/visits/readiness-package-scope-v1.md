# Meridian Marine Co. — Readiness Package Scope v1

## Harbour

Core readiness visibility:

- Confirm requested arrival/departure timing.
- Perform a basic readiness observation.
- Identify obvious blockers or material concerns.
- Communicate findings and coordinate separately approved work.

## Steward

Enhanced readiness support:

- Detailed readiness check.
- Review open actions.
- Coordinate approved vendors.
- Confirm presentation/readiness items.
- Provide owner-facing readiness summary.

## Reserve

High-touch readiness support:

- Proactive monitoring around planned use.
- Earlier readiness planning.
- Active tracking of preparation actions.
- Increased vendor coordination within authorization.
- Final readiness confirmation before planned arrival where practical.

## Private

Customized readiness program based on vessel, owner schedule, crew requirements, marina constraints, and requested level of support.

## Common exclusions

Fuel, repairs, specialist inspections, mechanical diagnosis, major provisioning, transportation, crew services, and other third-party work remain separate unless specifically included in a written agreement.
