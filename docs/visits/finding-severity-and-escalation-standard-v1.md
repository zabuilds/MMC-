# Meridian Marine Co. — Finding Severity & Escalation Standard v1

## Critical

Immediate threat to life, major vessel/property damage, significant active water ingress, fire/security emergency, or another condition requiring immediate escalation.

**Response:** escalate immediately through the applicable emergency/client procedure.

## Urgent

Material condition that could worsen quickly or materially affect vessel safety, security, readiness, or property if not addressed promptly.

**Response:** notify management/owner as appropriate and create a prioritized action.

## Attention

Meaningful condition requiring follow-up but not presenting an immediate critical or urgent risk.

**Response:** document, create an action where appropriate, and track to resolution.

## Monitor

Minor or stable condition that should remain visible in the vessel record but does not currently require active intervention.

**Response:** document and reassess on future visits.

## Escalation principle

Severity is based on observed condition, potential consequence, and reasonable urgency—not on speculation or unsupported diagnosis.

MMC personnel should describe what was observed and avoid presenting a specialist conclusion unless provided by an appropriately qualified professional.
