# Meridian Marine Co. — Arrival & Departure Readiness System v1

## Purpose

Turn routine vessel oversight into proactive preparation for owner arrival and departure while keeping MMC's role clearly defined.

## Owner Arrival Workflow

**REQUEST → PLAN → CHECK → COORDINATE → CONFIRM → REPORT**

### Request
Capture owner arrival date, expected use, requested preparation, and any special instructions.

### Plan
Review vessel status, latest visit, open findings/actions, vendor work, and required preparation window.

### Check
Perform an appropriate readiness visit covering safely observable condition, presentation, access, obvious issues, and agreed preparation items.

### Coordinate
Arrange approved vendors or service providers for authorized work.

### Confirm
Verify completed items to the extent appropriate to MMC's scope and identify anything remaining.

### Report
Provide the owner with a concise readiness status and any items requiring attention or approval.

## Departure Workflow

**NOTICE → CHECK → PREPARE → SECURE → CONFIRM → RECORD**

- Capture planned departure information.
- Confirm requested departure preparation.
- Review open issues and any known risks.
- Complete authorized preparation.
- Confirm systems/property are left according to agreed instructions.
- Restore access/security.
- Record outstanding matters and next oversight date.

## Readiness status

- **Ready** — agreed readiness items completed with no known material blocker.
- **Ready with Notes** — usable but one or more non-blocking matters remain.
- **Action Required** — preparation or correction remains before the owner's intended use.
- **Blocked** — a material issue prevents MMC from representing the vessel as ready.

## Boundary

MMC readiness confirmation is an operational observation within the agreed service scope. It is not a guarantee of seaworthiness, mechanical condition, legal compliance, or specialist certification.

## Timing principle

Readiness work should be planned far enough ahead to allow vendor coordination and owner decisions. Late requests may require expedited or third-party services and may not be fully achievable within the requested window.
