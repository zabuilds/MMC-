# Meridian Marine Co. — Package-Specific Visit Checklists v1

## Harbour — Core Oversight

Purpose: reliable recurring presence and early identification of material concerns.

Minimum checks:

- Exterior visual condition
- Visible water intrusion/leaks
- Bilge/standing water indicators where safely observable
- Security/access condition
- Obvious storm/weather impacts
- Visible abnormal conditions
- Basic vessel readiness indicators
- Evidence capture for material findings
- Report and action creation where required

## Steward — Enhanced Oversight

Includes Harbour checks plus:

- More detailed interior/exterior condition review
- Expanded systems indicators within safe observation limits
- Readiness review for owner arrival
- Follow-up on open actions
- Vendor coordination when authorized
- More detailed evidence capture
- Expanded report narrative

## Reserve — High-Touch Oversight

Includes Steward checks plus:

- Weekly recurring oversight
- Deeper review of vessel presentation/readiness
- Active monitoring of open actions
- Increased coordination with approved vendors where required
- Arrival/departure readiness support within agreed scope
- Priority identification of developing issues
- Enhanced reporting continuity

## Common boundary

No package automatically includes specialist diagnosis, engineering certification, marine surveying, repairs, or unlimited vendor coordination/spending. Those require separate authorization or specialist engagement where appropriate.
