# Meridian Marine Co. — Visit Closeout Standard v1

## Closeout sequence

**VERIFY → RECORD → REPORT → ESCALATE → ASSIGN → SCHEDULE → SECURE**

Before a visit is closed:

- Confirm the vessel was left in the intended condition.
- Confirm access/security was restored.
- Confirm required evidence was captured.
- Record findings and severity.
- Create required actions.
- Identify owner approvals needed.
- Record vendor follow-up where applicable.
- Prepare or queue the visit report.
- Escalate critical/urgent matters through the applicable procedure.
- Confirm the next visit or follow-up date where known.

## Quality check

A visit should not be marked complete merely because the operator left the vessel. The operational record must be sufficiently complete for another authorized MMC operator to understand what occurred and what happens next.

## Exceptions

If the visit cannot be completed as planned, record:

- What prevented completion
- What was completed
- Any immediate risk
- Required follow-up
- Owner/vendor communication
- Revised schedule where known
