# Meridian Marine Co. — Package Visit Scope Matrix v1

## Harbour — CI$225/month

Core recurring oversight for owners who need a dependable baseline presence.

Focus:
- One scheduled recurring vessel check
- Visible condition and security observations
- Basic evidence capture
- Findings and alerts
- Standard visit report
- Limited follow-up coordination within defined scope

## Steward — CI$395/month

Enhanced recurring oversight for owners who want more frequent attention and coordination.

Focus:
- Two scheduled recurring vessel checks
- Broader recurring observation coverage
- Standard evidence capture
- Findings and action tracking
- Standard visit reports
- Increased vendor/follow-up coordination within defined scope

## Reserve — CI$675/month

High-frequency oversight for owners requiring a stronger operational presence.

Focus:
- Weekly recurring vessel oversight
- Priority attention to emerging issues
- More frequent evidence and reporting
- Active action/vendor coordination within defined scope
- Arrival/readiness support where included by agreement
- Higher operational continuity and management visibility

## Private — Custom

For complex vessels, higher-touch requirements, unusual geography, or customized service commitments.

## Scope protection

Package frequency does not automatically mean unlimited coordination, emergency response, vendor spending, repairs, or specialist services. Those boundaries remain governed by the client agreement and add-on controls.
