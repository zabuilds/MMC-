# Meridian Marine Co. — System Outage & Recovery v1

## Outage response

**DETECT → DECLARE → PRIORITIZE → CONTINUE → COMMUNICATE → RESTORE → RECONCILE**

## During outage

Preserve the minimum operational record required for:

- Client/vessel identity
- Current location/status
- Critical findings
- Emergency contacts
- Active actions
- Vendor commitments
- Owner decisions
- Scheduled visits

Use an approved temporary method when the primary system is unavailable.

## Communication

If the outage materially affects service, communicate the limitation and expected next step without exposing unnecessary technical detail.

## Recovery

Once the primary system is restored:

- Reconcile temporary records.
- Verify material findings/actions.
- Confirm approvals and vendor commitments.
- Restore normal queue status.
- Check for duplicate or missing records.
- Record the outage and recovery where material.

## Recovery verification

Critical operational information must be independently checked before the incident is considered closed.
