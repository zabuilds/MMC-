# Meridian Marine Co. — Key Person & Coverage Standard v1

## Purpose

Prevent a single person's absence from becoming a client or vessel service failure.

## Coverage requirements

Every active client/vessel should have:

- A primary responsible operator
- A designated backup
- Accessible operating instructions
- Current access information under approved controls
- Emergency contacts
- Current open-action context
- Upcoming scheduled commitments

## Handoff

Before planned absence:

**REVIEW → HANDOFF → CONFIRM → COVER**

Review open matters, transfer responsibility, confirm the backup understands priorities, and record the coverage period.

## Unplanned absence

Management should identify the highest-priority active matters, assign backup coverage, and communicate only where the disruption materially affects service.

## Continuity principle

Client knowledge must live in the operating record rather than only in one person's memory, phone, or private notes.
