# Meridian Marine Co. — Business Continuity Framework v1

## Purpose

Keep essential client and vessel oversight functioning when normal operations are disrupted.

## Continuity priorities

1. Life and personnel safety
2. Critical vessel/property risks
3. Critical client communication
4. Emergency vendor coordination
5. Scheduled vessel oversight
6. Routine administration
7. Improvement work

## Disruption scenarios

The continuity plan must account for:

- Key-person unavailability
- Loss of application access
- Connectivity outage
- Vendor network failure
- Marina/access disruption
- Severe weather
- Vehicle/equipment failure
- Client communication failure
- Financial/payment interruption
- Data or security incident

## Continuity rule

When normal systems are unavailable, MMC should preserve the minimum information necessary to operate safely and communicate accurately, then reconcile temporary records into the authoritative system when service is restored.

## Recovery principle

Restore critical operations first. Do not allow routine administrative work to delay safety, emergency response, or material client communication.
