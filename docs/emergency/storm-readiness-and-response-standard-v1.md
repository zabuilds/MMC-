# Meridian Marine Co. — Storm Readiness & Response Standard v1

## Purpose

Provide a controlled process for protecting vessels, communicating with owners, coordinating approved vendors, and documenting conditions before and after severe weather.

## Operating phases

**MONITOR → PREPARE → SECURE → RESPOND → INSPECT → RECOVER → REPORT**

## Monitor

- Monitor relevant weather and marina conditions.
- Identify affected vessels and owners.
- Review vessel-specific storm instructions.
- Identify vendor and access constraints.

## Prepare

Where authorized and practical:

- Confirm vessel status.
- Identify exposed or vulnerable conditions visible to MMC.
- Coordinate approved preparation work.
- Confirm access and emergency contacts.
- Record owner decisions and limitations.

## Secure

- Complete authorized storm-preparation tasks.
- Coordinate approved vendors.
- Document material conditions before weather impact where practical.
- Confirm security/access arrangements.

## Respond

During or immediately after severe weather:

- Do not expose MMC personnel to unsafe conditions.
- Prioritize life safety.
- Follow marina/emergency instructions.
- Escalate material vessel emergencies through the appropriate emergency path.
- Coordinate vendors only when conditions permit.

## Inspect

After conditions are safe:

- Perform a post-storm visual assessment within MMC scope.
- Document visible damage and water intrusion.
- Capture evidence.
- Identify Critical/Urgent findings.
- Record access limitations.

## Recover

- Create actions for required follow-up.
- Coordinate authorized emergency or repair vendors.
- Track open work and owner approvals.
- Maintain a clear record of unresolved risks.

## Report

Provide owners with factual updates distinguishing:

- Observed conditions
- Vendor information
- Confirmed actions
- Unresolved matters
- Items requiring specialist assessment

## Safety boundary

MMC does not require personnel to enter unsafe areas or perform specialist emergency work. Life safety and safe access take priority over routine service commitments.
