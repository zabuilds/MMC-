# Meridian Marine Co. — Emergency Severity & Escalation v1

## Level 1 — Critical Emergency

Immediate threat to life, major active flooding, fire, significant security breach, sinking risk, or another condition requiring immediate emergency response.

**Priority:** life safety and emergency services first.

MMC should then notify the appropriate owner/management contact and coordinate specialist assistance where authorized and safely possible.

## Level 2 — Urgent Vessel Emergency

Material vessel condition requiring prompt intervention but not presenting an immediate life-safety emergency.

Examples may include significant water intrusion, major electrical failure, loss of critical systems, or serious storm damage.

**Priority:** protect vessel/property, notify appropriate parties, establish action, and obtain specialist/vendor response.

## Level 3 — Priority Operational Issue

A significant condition that should be addressed promptly but does not require emergency intervention.

**Priority:** document, communicate, assign action, and monitor.

## Level 4 — Routine Follow-up

Non-urgent condition suitable for normal service planning.

**Priority:** record and track through ordinary workflow.

## Escalation rule

Severity may be increased when conditions worsen, new information emerges, or a specialist/vendor identifies additional risk.

MMC personnel should not downgrade an emergency simply to fit routine workflow.
