# Meridian Marine Co. — Storm Client Communication Standard v1

## Purpose

Keep owners informed without creating unnecessary alarm, unsupported conclusions, or communication gaps during severe weather events.

## Communication phases

### Pre-storm

Communicate when appropriate:

- MMC's monitoring status
- Planned preparation activity
- Owner decisions required
- Known access/vendor limitations

### During event

Communicate only when safe and operationally useful. Do not imply on-site confirmation when MMC personnel cannot safely inspect the vessel.

### Post-storm

Once conditions permit:

- Confirm whether an assessment has occurred.
- State what was observed.
- Identify visible damage or concerns.
- Distinguish known facts from pending specialist assessment.
- Identify immediate actions and owner approvals.
- Provide the next expected update.

## Communication standard

Use calm, factual language. Avoid unsupported statements such as "everything is fine" when a vessel has not been inspected.

## No-response rule

If an owner cannot be reached during a material event, follow the authority and emergency provisions in the applicable client agreement and document reasonable attempts to communicate.
