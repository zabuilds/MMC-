# Meridian Marine Co. — Emergency Recovery Vendor Coordination v1

## Purpose

Move a post-storm or emergency finding into controlled third-party recovery without losing authorization, documentation, or accountability.

## Workflow

**FINDING → STABILIZE → SCOPE → AUTHORIZE → DISPATCH → UPDATE → VERIFY → RECONCILE**

### Finding
Record the observed condition, severity, evidence, and immediate risk.

### Stabilize
Where safe and authorized, coordinate measures that reduce ongoing damage.

### Scope
Define the work required and identify where specialist assessment is necessary.

### Authorize
Confirm owner approval or applicable pre-authorized emergency authority.

### Dispatch
Use the preferred emergency vendor first when available and appropriate; activate backup coverage when necessary.

### Update
Record vendor arrival, work status, unexpected conditions, scope changes, and updated costs.

### Verify
Confirm completion to MMC's operational scope and record any specialist certification or outstanding work separately.

### Reconcile
Close the vendor job, record final cost information, update actions/findings, and provide the owner with a recovery status.

## Escalation

Management review is required for material damage, significant cost exposure, disputed scope, safety concerns, insurance/claim-sensitive situations, or repeated vendor failure.
