# Meridian Marine Co. — Post-Storm Damage Documentation & Recovery v1

## Recovery sequence

**SAFE ACCESS → ASSESS → DOCUMENT → PRIORITIZE → NOTIFY → STABILIZE → REPAIR → VERIFY → CLOSE**

## Assess

Once safe access is confirmed, record visible conditions affecting:

- Hull and exterior
- Interior spaces
- Water intrusion
- Bilge/standing water indicators
- Electrical or visible systems indicators
- Security/access
- Dock or surrounding property where relevant
- Vessel contents where visibly affected

## Document

Capture factual evidence:

- Date/time
- Location
- Photos/video
- Description of visible damage
- Pre-existing condition where reasonably established
- New condition where reasonably established
- Access limitations
- Vendor observations

Do not alter evidence unnecessarily before documentation when doing so could compromise a claim or specialist assessment, unless immediate stabilization is required for safety or to prevent further damage.

## Prioritize

Classify recovery needs as:

1. Immediate emergency stabilization
2. Prevent further damage
3. Specialist assessment
4. Repair/restoration
5. Routine follow-up

## Notify

Communicate material findings to the appropriate owner/management contact and identify decisions or approvals required.

## Stabilize

Coordinate only authorized and appropriate measures to prevent further damage, using qualified specialists where necessary.

## Repair

Create tracked vendor jobs with defined scope, authorization, schedule, and cost information.

## Verify

MMC verifies visible completion within its agreed operational scope. Specialist certification remains the responsibility of the qualified specialist.

## Close

Close the recovery action only when:

- Work status is recorded.
- Remaining issues are identified.
- Required documentation is retained.
- Owner communication is complete.
- Follow-up responsibilities are assigned.
