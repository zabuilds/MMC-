# Meridian Marine Co. — Sales Pipeline & Qualification v1

## Pipeline

**LEAD → QUALIFIED → DISCOVERY → PROPOSAL → DECISION → WON/LOST → ONBOARDING**

## Qualification

Evaluate:

- Vessel ownership/profile
- Location and access
- Oversight need
- Absentee/seasonal ownership pattern
- Service expectations
- Urgency
- Fit with MMC operating scope
- Commercial fit

## Discovery

Understand the owner's current pain, existing support structure, desired oversight level, communication preferences, and recurring concerns.

## Proposal

Present the appropriate package or custom scope without inventing services outside the approved MMC model.

## Decision

Track the decision, objections, requested changes, and next action.

## Sales discipline

Do not treat every lead as a fit. A qualified prospect should have a real problem MMC can solve and a service expectation compatible with MMC's operating model.
