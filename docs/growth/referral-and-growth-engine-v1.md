# Meridian Marine Co. — Referral & Growth Engine v1

## Growth channels

Prioritize channels that reinforce trust and reach qualified vessel owners:

- Existing client referrals
- Marina relationships
- Marine service providers
- Yacht professionals
- Realtors/property professionals where relevant
- Private owner networks
- Direct outreach to qualified prospects

## Referral flow

**SOURCE → INTRODUCTION → QUALIFICATION → DISCOVERY → PROPOSAL → CONVERSION → THANK/UPDATE**

## Partner principle

Referral relationships should preserve MMC's independence. A partner may introduce the owner, but MMC remains responsible for defining and delivering its own scope.

## Growth metrics

Track:

- Leads by source
- Qualified opportunities
- Proposal rate
- Conversion rate
- Sales cycle length
- Revenue by source
- Referral contribution
- Client retention by acquisition source

## Growth discipline

Growth should not outrun operational capacity. New client acquisition must be balanced against visit capacity, vendor coverage, response expectations, and service quality.
