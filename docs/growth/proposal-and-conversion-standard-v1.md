# Meridian Marine Co. — Proposal & Conversion Standard v1

## Proposal structure

A proposal should clearly show:

- Client/vessel understanding
- Recommended service level
- Included services
- Visit cadence
- Communication/reporting expectations
- Coordination boundaries
- Pricing
- Start requirements
- Next step

## Conversion flow

**PROPOSAL → QUESTIONS → REFINEMENT → APPROVAL → AGREEMENT → PAYMENT/SETUP → ONBOARDING**

## Commercial discipline

Do not discount or expand scope casually. Any material change should be recorded so the commercial agreement and operating scope remain aligned.

## Lost opportunities

Record the reason when known:

- Price
- Timing
- Not a fit
- Existing provider
- Delayed decision
- Vessel sold/transferred
- Other known reason

Lost opportunities should feed back into pricing, positioning, qualification, and service design decisions.
