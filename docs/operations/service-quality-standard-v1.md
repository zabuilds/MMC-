# Meridian Marine Co. — Service Quality Standard v1

## Purpose

Define the standard MMC must meet on every client interaction and vessel visit.

## Five quality principles

### 1. Reliable
Scheduled work happens as promised or the owner is informed promptly when circumstances require a change.

### 2. Documented
Material observations and actions leave a clear operational record.

### 3. Responsive
Critical and urgent matters receive the appropriate escalation without unnecessary delay.

### 4. Professional
MMC personnel remain calm, respectful, appropriately presented, and careful with vessels and owner property.

### 5. Bounded
MMC never creates false expectations about technical expertise, emergency capability, captaincy, repairs, or third-party performance.

## Visit quality checks

Before closing a visit, confirm:

- Correct vessel and visit scope.
- Inspection completed according to package standard.
- Findings classified.
- Material evidence captured where appropriate.
- Required escalation completed.
- Open actions recorded.
- Report complete.
- Next action identified.

## Client experience checks

At meaningful service milestones, confirm:

- Owner knows current vessel status.
- Outstanding decisions are clear.
- Vendor/service actions are visible.
- Readiness requirements are understood.
- No material request is sitting without ownership.

## Quality failure response

When MMC misses a commitment or identifies an operational error:

1. Acknowledge it.
2. Correct it where possible.
3. Notify the affected owner when material.
4. Record the cause.
5. Update the process if the issue reveals a repeatable weakness.

## Continuous improvement

Early-client feedback and operating data should be reviewed regularly to improve package scope, visit standards, communication, vendor processes, staffing, and pricing.
