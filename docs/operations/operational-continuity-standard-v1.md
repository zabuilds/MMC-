# Meridian Marine Co. — Operational Continuity Standard v1

## Purpose

Maintain reliable client service when an individual team member, vendor, system, vehicle, or normal operating condition becomes unavailable.

## Continuity priorities

1. Safety and critical vessel matters.
2. Critical client communications.
3. Scheduled vessel oversight.
4. Time-sensitive readiness commitments.
5. Urgent vendor coordination.
6. Routine administration.

## Single-person dependency rule

Material vessel knowledge should live in the approved vessel record rather than exclusively in one person's memory, phone, or messages.

## Handoff standard

When work changes hands, the receiving person should have:

- Vessel/client identity
- Current package
- Current issue/action status
- Last visit/report
- Open approvals
- Vendor contacts
- Next action/date
- Relevant access information through approved secure methods

## Service disruption

If MMC cannot deliver a scheduled service as promised:

1. Identify the affected clients.
2. Prioritize by urgency and package commitment.
3. Notify affected owners when material.
4. Reschedule or assign alternate capacity.
5. Record the disruption.
6. Review the cause and prevention.

## System outage

If a normal digital system becomes unavailable, use an approved temporary record method and reconcile the information into the primary system once restored.

## Recovery principle

Continuity planning exists to protect client confidence and vessel oversight—not to create unnecessary bureaucracy. Keep contingency procedures practical and usable.
