# Meridian Marine Co. — Operating Day Workflow v1

## Purpose

Define how MMC turns scheduled work and incoming issues into controlled operational actions.

## Daily control loop

**PLAN → CHECK → EXECUTE → ESCALATE → COORDINATE → CLOSE → REVIEW**

### Plan
Review today's vessel visits, open maintenance items, vendor appointments, owner requests, weather/event conditions, and access constraints.

### Check
Confirm each job has a clear objective, package scope, location, access, and required information.

### Execute
Complete the applicable inspection, readiness, coordination, or follow-up task using the approved SOP.

### Escalate
Immediately identify Critical/Urgent issues and follow the escalation standard.

### Coordinate
Assign or contact the appropriate qualified party when owner approval and package authority permit.

### Close
Complete the report, evidence, action status, and next-step record.

### Review
At the end of the operating cycle, review unresolved issues and carry them forward deliberately.

## Daily priority order

1. Safety and emergency matters.
2. Critical vessel/property risk.
3. Time-sensitive owner arrival/departure readiness.
4. Scheduled vessel visits.
5. Urgent maintenance/vendor actions.
6. Routine coordination and reporting.
7. Administrative cleanup.

## No orphan work rule

Every material task must have an owner, status, next action, and next date. Verbal or informal requests should be converted into a trackable operational record.
