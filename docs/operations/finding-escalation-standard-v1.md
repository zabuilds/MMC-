# Meridian Marine Co. — Finding & Escalation Standard v1

## Purpose

Ensure MMC responds consistently when a vessel visit identifies a condition requiring attention.

## Decision path

**OBSERVE → CLASSIFY → DOCUMENT → ESCALATE → COORDINATE → VERIFY → CLOSE**

### Observe
Identify the condition without diagnosing beyond MMC's role.

### Classify
Assign Critical, Urgent, Attention, or Monitor.

### Document
Capture the facts, location, evidence, and relevant context.

### Escalate
Notify the appropriate owner/emergency contact according to severity and the agreed communication path.

### Coordinate
When authorized, engage the appropriate qualified third party and track the response.

### Verify
Confirm that the reported action occurred or obtain an appropriate status update.

### Close
Record outcome, remaining risk, and whether continued monitoring is required.

## Critical findings

Examples include visible major flooding, active significant leak, fire/smoke indicators, major security breach, severe storm damage, or another condition presenting immediate material risk.

Actions:
- Escalate immediately.
- Follow the owner's emergency contact hierarchy.
- Contact emergency or marina resources where appropriate and authorized.
- Do not delay escalation to complete a report.
- Document actions after immediate safety concerns are addressed.

## Urgent findings

Examples include significant water intrusion, failing access/security condition, substantial equipment abnormality, or damage likely to worsen materially.

Actions:
- Notify owner promptly.
- Recommend qualified third-party attention.
- Coordinate where included and authorized.
- Track until an appropriate status is obtained.

## Attention findings

Examples include developing corrosion, minor leaks, maintenance items, wear, or conditions that should be addressed during planned service.

Actions:
- Include in report.
- Recommend or coordinate service according to package.
- Add to follow-up tracking where applicable.

## Monitor findings

Minor, unchanged, or informational observations.

Actions:
- Include in routine reporting when useful.
- Continue observation on subsequent visits.

## Communication rules

- Critical issues are communicated immediately.
- Urgent issues are communicated promptly.
- Attention and Monitor items normally appear in the scheduled report unless circumstances justify earlier contact.
- MMC communicates observations as observations, not technical diagnoses.
- Owner approval is required for non-emergency third-party expenditure unless a separate written authority exists.
- All material coordination actions should leave an auditable record.
