# Meridian Marine Co. — Operational Exception Alerts v1

## Purpose

Ensure management sees exceptions that can materially affect clients, vessels, safety, or financial performance.

## Immediate alerts

- Critical finding
- Emergency event
- Major security incident
- Significant active water intrusion
- Material vessel damage
- Safety-related service interruption

## Priority alerts

- Urgent finding
- Readiness blocked
- Material owner approval overdue
- Vendor failure affecting a deadline
- Material action overdue
- Repeated failed visits

## Management alerts

- Vendor coverage gap
- Growing backlog
- Aging blocked work
- Client complaint pattern
- Margin deterioration
- Unusual service/vendor cost
- Concentration risk

## Alert discipline

Alerts should be actionable. Each alert should identify the affected client/vessel, reason, current status, owner, and next required action.

Resolved alerts should remain available in history for review and trend analysis.
