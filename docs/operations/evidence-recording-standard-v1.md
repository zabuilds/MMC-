# Meridian Marine Co. — Evidence Recording Standard v1

## Purpose

Ensure photos, video, and written observations are useful, consistent, and tied to the vessel record.

## Evidence principles

Evidence should be:

- Relevant
- Clear
- Dated
- Associated with the correct vessel
- Associated with a specific finding when applicable
- Stored in the approved MMC record system

## Photo standard

When practical:

1. Capture a context image showing location.
2. Capture a closer image showing the condition.
3. Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
4. Do not manipulate images in a way that changes their meaning.

## Video standard

Use video when movement, sound, sequence, or wider context materially improves understanding. Keep clips purposeful rather than excessive.

## Written observation standard

Describe what was observed, where it was observed, and why it matters. Avoid unsupported technical diagnosis.

## Evidence quality check

Before attaching evidence, confirm:

- Correct vessel
- Correct location/context
- Clear enough to interpret
- Relevant to the finding
- No unnecessary sensitive information

## Retention principle

Evidence should remain linked to the relevant visit, finding, or action so an authorized MMC team member can understand the historical record later.
