# Meridian Marine Co. — Field Kit & Equipment Standard v1

## Purpose

Define a practical baseline field kit that supports safe, professional vessel oversight without turning MMC into a repair operation.

## Core field kit

- Smartphone/camera suitable for clear documentation
- Charged power bank
- Flashlight/headlamp
- Basic personal protective equipment appropriate to the visit
- Non-marking footwear appropriate to vessel access
- Gloves appropriate to task
- Basic measuring/tape tools where useful for documentation
- Notebook or approved digital record method
- Identification and MMC contact information
- Weather-appropriate personal protection

## Optional documentation equipment

Depending on vessel and assignment:

- Higher-quality camera
- Tablet
- Moisture-observation equipment used only within training and stated limitations
- Simple non-invasive measurement tools

## Equipment boundaries

Field equipment is for observation, documentation, safety, and basic non-invasive verification. Equipment must not be used to perform specialist diagnosis unless MMC has appropriately trained and authorized personnel.

## Equipment readiness

Before each operating day:

- Charge electronic equipment.
- Confirm storage capacity.
- Confirm camera lens is clean.
- Confirm PPE is available and serviceable.
- Replace damaged or expired safety equipment.
- Confirm required equipment for scheduled assignments.
