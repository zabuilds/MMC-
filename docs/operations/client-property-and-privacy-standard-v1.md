# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Property & Privacy Standard v1

## Purpose

Protect client property, vessel information, personal information, and operational details throughout MMC service delivery.

## Property handling

MMC personnel should:

- Treat all client property as private and valuable.
- Move property only when necessary for an authorized task.
- Document material damage or unusual conditions promptly.
- Never use client property for personal purposes.
- Never remove property from a vessel without explicit authorization.

## Information handling

Limit access to information according to operational need. Avoid unnecessary sharing of:

- Owner contact details
- Vessel access information
- Security details
- Travel schedules
- Arrival/departure plans
- Financial information
- Private communications
- Sensitive vessel documentation

## Reporting privacy

Reports should contain information relevant to the service delivered. Avoid including unnecessary personal details or sensitive access credentials.

## Photography/privacy

Photos and video should focus on vessel condition and relevant service evidence. Avoid capturing people, private documents, screens, or unrelated personal information where reasonably avoidable.

## Communication privacy

Use approved MMC communication channels for client matters. Verify recipient identity before sending sensitive information.

## Third-party sharing

Share client/vessel information with vendors only when necessary to perform an authorized service. Share the minimum information reasonably required.

## Privacy incident

Suspected unauthorized disclosure, lost sensitive information, or inappropriate access should be treated as an incident and escalated to MMC management.
