# Meridian Marine Co. — Storm & Emergency Response Standard v1

## Purpose

Define a calm, repeatable response framework for storm events and vessel emergencies while keeping MMC within its role and authority.

## Response model

**ASSESS → PROTECT → ESCALATE → COORDINATE → DOCUMENT → VERIFY**

### Assess
Determine whether conditions are safe to observe and identify the apparent level of risk.

### Protect
Where safely and explicitly authorized, support reasonable protective coordination. Do not place MMC personnel in unsafe conditions.

### Escalate
Use the vessel's emergency contact hierarchy and appropriate emergency/marina resources when required.

### Coordinate
Coordinate qualified third parties, marina personnel, service providers, or other responsible parties within package scope and authority.

### Document
Record observations, actions, contacts, timestamps, and unresolved risks.

### Verify
When safe and practical, confirm the coordinated action occurred and communicate remaining concerns.

## Storm readiness

Before a forecasted significant storm/event, MMC may coordinate within package scope:

- Owner readiness instructions
- Marina/vendor communication
- Securing arrangements with responsible qualified parties
- Removal/protection of agreed movable items by responsible parties
- Fuel/service scheduling where appropriate
- Access and contact confirmation
- Post-storm inspection scheduling

MMC does not guarantee vessel survival, weather outcomes, marina actions, or third-party performance.

## Emergency boundaries

MMC does not replace emergency services, marina emergency operations, captains, crew, licensed trades, or specialist marine responders.

MMC personnel should not:

- Enter unsafe conditions
- Perform specialist rescue or repair
- Operate unfamiliar equipment in a hazardous situation
- Represent an observation as a technical diagnosis
- Commit substantial owner expenditure without authority

## Priority handling

### Critical
Immediate escalation and safety-first response.

### Urgent
Prompt owner notification and qualified third-party coordination.

### Attention
Track and coordinate through normal operating workflow.

### Monitor
Document and continue observation.

## Package application

Harbour: storm/emergency coordination is generally an add-on or separately defined service unless explicitly included in the active agreement.

Steward: defined storm/event and emergency coordination within the agreed operating scope.

Reserve: priority storm/event and emergency coordination within the agreed operating scope.

## After-action review

Following a significant event, MMC should record:

- What occurred
- What was observed
- What actions were coordinated
- Who was contacted
- What remains unresolved
- What preventive action is recommended
- Whether the owner's service plan should be updated
