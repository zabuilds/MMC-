# Meridian Marine Co. — Vessel Record Standard v1

## Purpose

Define the minimum operational record required for every active MMC vessel.

## Core vessel record

- Vessel name
- Vessel type/class
- Registration/identifier when appropriate
- Home marina/location
- Owner and authorized contacts
- Emergency contacts
- Access instructions
- Package and service start date
- Visit schedule
- Reporting preferences
- Owner arrival/departure patterns
- Preferred vendors
- Known vessel conditions
- Baseline date
- Open maintenance/actions
- Recent service history
- Storm/event notes
- Special instructions

## Operational history

Each vessel should maintain a chronological history of:

- Visits
- Reports
- Findings
- Evidence references
- Vendor coordination
- Maintenance actions
- Owner decisions
- Readiness events
- Emergency/storm events
- Closure outcomes

## Data quality rule

If a material operational fact changes, update the vessel record promptly. The current vessel record should be sufficient for another authorized MMC team member to understand the vessel without relying on personal memory.

## Privacy and access

Vessel and owner information should be limited to personnel and service providers who need it for the relevant task. Sensitive access instructions should not be placed in unnecessary reports or broadly shared communications.
