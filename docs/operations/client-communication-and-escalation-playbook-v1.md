# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Communication & Escalation Playbook v1

## Objective

Give clients timely, clear, calm communication while ensuring material issues receive the right level of attention.

## Communication principles

Every client communication should be:

- Clear
- Accurate
- Concise
- Evidence-based
- Action-oriented
- Appropriate to the severity

## Routine update

Use when service is proceeding normally.

Structure:

**VISIT STATUS → KEY OBSERVATION → ACTION/STATUS → NEXT STEP**

## Routine finding

Use when an issue is identified but does not require immediate escalation.

Structure:

**WHAT WE FOUND → WHY IT MATTERS → EVIDENCE → RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP → OWNER DECISION IF REQUIRED**

## Urgent communication

Use when prompt attention is required.

Structure:

**URGENT → WHAT HAPPENED → CURRENT CONDITION → IMMEDIATE ACTION → WHAT MMC NEEDS → NEXT UPDATE**

Do not wait for the scheduled report.

## Critical communication

Use for immediate threats or major events.

Structure:

**CRITICAL → IMMEDIATE FACTS → SAFETY/PROTECTION ACTION → WHO HAS BEEN NOTIFIED → OWNER DECISION REQUIRED → NEXT UPDATE**

Escalate through the appropriate emergency/responsible channels first when required.

## Client approval request

Clearly separate:

- What was observed
- What work is being proposed
- Why it is needed
- Expected cost if known
- What MMC is asking the owner to approve
- What happens if approval is delayed

Never imply approval where it has not been received.

## Vendor delay

If a vendor misses a commitment:

**STATUS → IMPACT → FOLLOW-UP/ESCALATION → ALTERNATIVE → CLIENT DECISION IF REQUIRED → NEXT UPDATE**

## Report delivery

When sending a routine report, highlight only the items requiring attention rather than forcing the client to discover them inside the document.

## No-news communication

Where the service agreement calls for it, a concise confirmation that the scheduled check was completed and no material issues were identified can itself provide value.

## Escalation ladder

**NORMAL → ROUTINE FINDING → URGENT → CRITICAL**

Severity determines communication speed and channel.

## Follow-up standard

When a material issue remains open, communicate progress until the item is verified and closed.

Silence is not closure.

## Documentation

Material client communications should be recorded against the relevant vessel/finding/action so the operational history remains complete.

## Principle

MMC should make owners feel informed and in control—not overwhelmed, alarmed, or forced to manage the details themselves.
