# Meridian Marine Co. — Management Dashboard v1

## Executive view

The dashboard should answer five questions:

1. Are our clients being serviced reliably?
2. Are important vessel issues being handled?
3. Are owners waiting on us or vendors?
4. Is the vendor network healthy?
5. Is the business financially healthy?

## Dashboard sections

### Client health

- Active clients/vessels
- Retention
- Service completion
- Outstanding owner approvals
- Client escalations

### Vessel health

- Critical/Urgent findings
- Aging actions
- Readiness status
- Open recovery work

### Operations

- Today's queue
- Overdue work
- Blocked work
- Upcoming visits
- Upcoming arrivals/departures

### Vendor network

- Coverage gaps
- Active vendor jobs
- Delays
- Service failures
- Preferred vendor performance

### Financial

- Recurring revenue
- Revenue by package
- Revenue per vessel
- Direct service/vendor costs
- Gross margin indicators

## Alerting

The dashboard should surface exceptions rather than forcing management to inspect every record manually.

Priority alerts should include:

- Critical/Urgent findings
- Overdue material actions
- Blocked readiness
- Vendor failure
- Approval bottlenecks
- Service delivery exceptions
- Material financial anomalies
