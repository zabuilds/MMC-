# Meridian Marine Co. — Master Operating Manual v1

## Purpose

Define the standard operating rhythm for delivering MMC vessel oversight consistently, professionally, and independently.

## Operating promise

**WATCH → DOCUMENT → ALERT → COORDINATE → VERIFY → REPORT → CLOSE**

## 1. Client onboarding

**CLOSED → WELCOME → COLLECT → VERIFY → PLAN → BASELINE → ACTIVATE**

A vessel is not treated as active until the required information, access, communication path, and operating plan are sufficiently verified.

## 2. Scheduled visit

**PREPARE → ARRIVE → INSPECT → DOCUMENT → ASSESS → ESCALATE → COMPLETE**

Operators follow the approved visit scope and record relevant evidence and limitations.

## 3. Findings

Every material finding receives:

- Observation
- Evidence
- Location
- Severity
- Immediate response if required
- Recommended next step
- Assignment/follow-up requirement

## 4. Critical and urgent escalation

Critical matters are escalated immediately through the approved communication path.

Urgent matters are routed according to the service standard and monitored until acknowledged.

The portal/report is not a substitute for immediate emergency communication.

## 5. Vendor coordination

**FINDING → SCOPE → APPROVAL IF REQUIRED → SELECT → ASSIGN → ACKNOWLEDGE → PROGRESS → COMPLETE → VERIFY → CLOSE**

MMC retains oversight and does not represent itself as the technical contractor unless separately qualified and authorized.

## 6. Reporting

**DRAFT → QA → APPROVE → DELIVER → FOLLOW UP**

Reports should lead with vessel status, material changes, actions, and readiness, followed by supporting evidence.

## 7. Follow-up

Open actions remain visible until their required verification and communication steps are complete.

Vendor completion alone does not close an MMC operational item.

## 8. Client communication

Communication should be:

- Calm
- Clear
- Evidence-backed
- Timely
- Appropriate to severity
- Free of unsupported technical conclusions

## 9. Quality assurance

QA checks should confirm:

- Correct vessel/client
- Visit completeness
- Evidence quality
- Finding severity
- Report accuracy
- Required limitations disclosed
- Actions correctly assigned
- Critical/urgent escalation handled

## 10. Operational exceptions

Exceptions include blocked visits, access problems, missed vendors, incomplete work, unexpected scope, failed verification, automation failure, and system outages.

Exception flow:

**DETECT → ASSESS → COMMUNICATE → CORRECT/REASSIGN → VERIFY → CLOSE**

## 11. Business continuity

If systems are unavailable, maintain a manual operational record and emergency communication path. Reconcile offline work when systems recover.

## 12. Daily operating rhythm

### Start of day

Review:

- Today's visits
- Critical/Urgent findings
- Overdue actions
- Client approvals
- Vendor exceptions
- Reports requiring attention

### During day

Prioritize safety, active visits, material findings, time-sensitive decisions, and service commitments.

### End of day

Confirm:

- Visits completed or properly carried forward
- Findings recorded
- Critical/Urgent items escalated
- Actions assigned
- Reports queued
- Exceptions visible

## 13. Management rhythm

Review service quality, workload, capacity, client issues, vendor performance, and financial indicators regularly.

## 14. Core rule

MMC is paid to provide **independent oversight and confidence**. Every operating decision should preserve that position.
