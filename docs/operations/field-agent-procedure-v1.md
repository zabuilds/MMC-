# Meridian Marine Co. — Field Agent Procedure v1

## Purpose

Give every MMC field operator a consistent method for safe, professional vessel visits.

## Before leaving

- Review vessel record and previous unresolved findings.
- Confirm package and visit objective.
- Confirm access and marina requirements.
- Review weather and relevant event conditions.
- Confirm required phone, camera, PPE, and basic visit equipment.

## At the vessel

- Confirm correct vessel and safe access.
- Conduct the applicable inspection checklist.
- Never enter unsafe areas.
- Avoid disturbing owner property unnecessarily.
- Record observations factually.
- Capture evidence only where useful.
- Escalate Critical/Urgent findings promptly.

## Professional conduct

Field agents represent MMC and should be:

- Punctual
- Calm
- Respectful
- Discreet
- Professionally presented
- Careful with owner property
- Clear about what they know and do not know

## Prohibited conduct

Field agents must not:

- Diagnose specialist failures beyond their qualifications.
- Perform unauthorized repairs.
- Make commitments to vendors or owners outside authority.
- Share owner/vessel information unnecessarily.
- Use owner property for personal purposes.
- Leave a material issue undocumented.

## Visit closeout

Before leaving:

- Confirm all required observations are captured.
- Secure agreed access points.
- Confirm no tools/equipment are left behind.
- Record follow-up actions.
- Escalate material issues.
- Submit visit record for reporting.
