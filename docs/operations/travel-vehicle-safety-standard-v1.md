# Meridian Marine Co. — Travel & Vehicle Safety Standard v1

## Purpose

Keep travel between vessels efficient, professional, and safe while protecting client property and MMC equipment.

## Travel planning

Before travel:

- Confirm vessel location and access point.
- Allow realistic travel and parking time.
- Consider weather, traffic, marina access, and event conditions.
- Group nearby visits where practical without compromising promised service timing.
- Carry only equipment required for the assignment.

## Vehicle standard

MMC vehicles used for operations should be:

- Roadworthy
- Clean and professional
- Secure for equipment
- Maintained on a documented schedule
- Appropriately insured for the intended use

## Arrival/departure

- Park only in authorized or appropriate areas.
- Follow marina/property rules.
- Do not block access or emergency routes.
- Secure equipment before leaving the vehicle.
- Confirm client property and access items are accounted for after the visit.

## Weather and travel safety

Do not require a field agent to travel or access a vessel when conditions create an unreasonable safety risk. Reschedule or escalate when appropriate.

## Incident response

Vehicle accidents, property damage, lost access items, or other material travel incidents must be reported and recorded under the MMC incident procedure.
