# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Report Structure v1

## Report purpose

Every MMC report should give the owner a fast, calm, decision-useful picture of the vessel without unnecessary technical language.

## Standard report structure

### 1. Visit summary
- Vessel
- Visit date/time
- MMC service tier
- Overall status
- Short owner-facing summary

### 2. Priority status
Use a simple status hierarchy:

- **Critical:** immediate material risk requiring immediate escalation.
- **Urgent:** prompt action recommended.
- **Attention:** action or tracking recommended.
- **Monitor:** no immediate action; continue observation.

### 3. Vessel condition
Summarize notable observations across:

- Exterior
- Interior
- Water/moisture
- Security/access
- Visible equipment/status indicators
- Environmental conditions
- Other agreed inspection areas

### 4. Evidence
Attach clear photos/video where they materially improve understanding.

Evidence should be tied to the relevant finding rather than presented as an undifferentiated photo dump.

### 5. Open actions
For each open item:

- Finding
- Priority
- Recommended next step
- Owner decision required, if any
- Vendor/service party, if applicable
- Current status
- Next follow-up date

### 6. Readiness status
When applicable:

- Owner arrival readiness
- Departure readiness
- Fuel/service status
- Vendor/service status
- Outstanding blockers

### 7. Owner decisions
Clearly identify anything requiring approval, instruction, or additional authorization.

### 8. Next step
End with the next meaningful action or confirmation date.

## Reporting tone

MMC reports should be:

- Calm
- Concise
- Factual
- Professional
- Evidence-led
- Action-oriented

Avoid alarmist language, unnecessary technical terminology, unsupported diagnoses, and vague statements.

## Package differentiation

Harbour: concise report focused on condition and material findings.

Steward: enhanced report including maintenance/service action tracking.

Reserve: premium report including broader action priorities, readiness status, and unresolved-risk visibility.
