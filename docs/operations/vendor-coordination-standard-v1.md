# Meridian Marine Co. — Vendor Coordination Standard v1

## Purpose

Provide owners with controlled coordination of qualified third parties without making MMC the repair provider, technical diagnostician, captain, or yacht manager.

## Coordination flow

**IDENTIFY → AUTHORIZE → SOURCE/CONTACT → SCHEDULE → CONFIRM → VERIFY → REPORT**

### Identify
Define the issue, requested service, urgency, vessel/location, and desired outcome.

### Authorize
Confirm owner approval, package scope, spending authority, and any applicable emergency authority before committing cost.

### Source/contact
Use the owner's preferred provider where available. Otherwise identify an appropriate qualified third party without representing MMC as the technical expert.

### Schedule
Coordinate access, timing, marina requirements, and relevant owner constraints.

### Confirm
Confirm appointment, responsible party, expected service, and any known cost estimate where available.

### Verify
Confirm attendance and observable completion. Verification is not technical certification of workmanship.

### Report
Record the vendor, service, date, status, cost/estimate if available, unresolved items, and next action.

## Vendor record requirements

For recurring or material vendors, maintain:

- Company/person name
- Service category
- Contact details
- Vessel(s) served
- Owner preference/status
- Last service date
- Current open work
- Next scheduled action
- Notes on access or marina requirements
- Relevant insurance/licensing information when provided and appropriate to MMC's process

## Owner approval rules

- Routine included coordination may proceed within agreed package scope.
- New third-party expenditure requires owner approval unless a documented emergency authority applies.
- MMC should not guarantee vendor pricing, availability, workmanship, or completion time.
- MMC should disclose known conflicts or limitations when relevant.

## Vendor performance tracking

MMC should track simple operational signals:

- Response reliability
- Appointment reliability
- Communication quality
- Completion status
- Recurring unresolved issues
- Owner satisfaction/concerns

This is an internal operating aid and does not replace professional vendor qualification.
