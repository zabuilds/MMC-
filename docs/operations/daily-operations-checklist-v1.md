# Meridian Marine Co. — Daily Operations Checklist v1

## Start of day

- [ ] Review today's visits
- [ ] Review Critical/Urgent findings
- [ ] Review overdue actions
- [ ] Review pending client approvals
- [ ] Review vendor exceptions
- [ ] Review reports awaiting attention
- [ ] Confirm staffing/capacity coverage

## During operations

- [ ] Follow visit scope
- [ ] Capture required evidence
- [ ] Record findings promptly
- [ ] Escalate Critical matters immediately
- [ ] Assign required actions
- [ ] Track vendor commitments
- [ ] Preserve client communication history

## End of day

- [ ] Confirm visits completed or carried forward correctly
- [ ] Confirm findings have owners/statuses
- [ ] Confirm Critical/Urgent items were acknowledged
- [ ] Confirm actions are scheduled
- [ ] Confirm reports are queued appropriately
- [ ] Confirm exceptions remain visible
- [ ] Confirm no operational work was silently lost
