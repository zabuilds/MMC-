# Meridian Marine Co. — Visit Execution & Reporting System v1

## Objective

Make every scheduled vessel visit consistent, evidence-based, efficient, and easy for the owner to understand.

## Visit lifecycle

**SCHEDULE → PREPARE → ARRIVE → ACCESS → OBSERVE → DOCUMENT → ASSESS → ESCALATE → COMPLETE → REPORT → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE**

## 1. Prepare

Before departure:

- Confirm vessel, location, and visit scope
- Review baseline and prior visit
- Review open findings/actions
- Review vendor activity
- Confirm access arrangements
- Confirm required equipment
- Identify known priorities

## 2. Arrive & verify

- Confirm correct vessel/location
- Record arrival time
- Confirm access condition
- Note immediate visible exceptions
- Begin evidence capture

## 3. Observe

Work through the approved service scope systematically.

Compare relevant observations against:

- Baseline
- Previous visit
- Open actions
- Known vessel-specific watch points

## 4. Document

For material observations capture:

- Date/time
- Location/component
- Description
- Evidence
- Comparison/change context
- Severity
- Recommended next action where appropriate

## 5. Assess

Classify observations consistently:

**CRITICAL → URGENT → OPERATIONAL → ADMINISTRATIVE**

Clearly distinguish observations from technical conclusions that require a qualified specialist.

## 6. Escalate

Critical and urgent matters follow the incident/escalation workflow immediately rather than waiting for the routine report.

## 7. Complete visit

Before leaving:

- Confirm required areas were covered
- Confirm evidence is complete
- Confirm immediate actions are recorded
- Confirm vendor/client follow-ups are assigned
- Record departure time

## 8. Report

The report should be structured for rapid owner comprehension:

**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY → KEY FINDINGS → EVIDENCE → ACTIONS → VENDOR/COORDINATION STATUS → ITEMS REQUIRING OWNER DECISION → NEXT VISIT WATCH POINTS**

## 9. Report QA

Before delivery verify:

- Correct vessel/client
- Correct visit date
- Evidence matches finding
- Severity is appropriate
- No unsupported claims
- Actions have owners/status
- Client decisions are clearly identified
- Report is complete and readable

## 10. Follow-up & closure

A finding remains open until the required action, evidence, and verification are complete.

**OBSERVE → ACT → VERIFY → CLOSE**

## Visit quality metrics

Track:

- Visit completion rate
- Average visit duration
- Report turnaround
- Rework rate
- Findings per visit
- Critical/Urgent response performance
- Open/overdue actions
- Client questions after report delivery

## Efficiency rule

Do not optimize visits by reducing evidence quality. Optimize through preparation, repeatable check paths, better scheduling, clear scope, and accumulated vessel knowledge.

## Principle

The report is not the product by itself. The product is the owner's confidence that the vessel is being watched, changes are being identified, and important issues are being moved toward resolution.
