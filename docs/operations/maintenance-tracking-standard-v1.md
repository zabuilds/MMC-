# Meridian Marine Co. — Maintenance Tracking Standard v1

## Purpose

Create a single operational view of vessel issues and planned service so nothing important disappears between visits.

## Action record

Each tracked item should contain:

- Vessel
- Finding/service need
- Priority
- Date identified
- Source visit/report
- Owner decision required
- Approved/not approved status
- Vendor/service party
- Estimated or approved cost when available
- Scheduled date
- Current status
- Last update
- Next action/date
- Completion evidence or confirmation
- Closure notes

## Statuses

**New → Awaiting Owner → Approved → Scheduled → In Progress → Verification → Closed**

Optional exception status: **On Hold** when an item cannot progress because of owner, vendor, marina, weather, parts, access, or another documented dependency.

## Priority rules

Critical and Urgent findings receive active follow-up until an appropriate response or owner decision is documented.

Attention items are tracked according to package scope and planned maintenance needs.

Monitor items remain in the vessel history and are reviewed during future visits.

## Steward expectation

Steward should maintain the recurring maintenance/action picture and follow up on open routine items.

## Reserve expectation

Reserve should maintain a more proactive action picture, prioritize unresolved material items, and provide higher-frequency status visibility.

## Closure rule

An item is not closed merely because a vendor was contacted. Closure requires reasonable confirmation that the intended action occurred, or an explicit owner decision to close, defer, or accept the remaining condition.
