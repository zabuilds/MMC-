# Meridian Marine Co. — Service Quality Assurance & Audit System v1

## Objective

Keep MMC service delivery consistent as vessel count, operators, vendors, and client volume increase.

## Quality layers

### Layer 1 — Visit-level QA

Every completed visit is checked for:

- Correct vessel/client
- Scope completion
- Required evidence
- Accurate observations
- Appropriate severity
- Correct timestamps
- Clear actions
- Correct escalation

### Layer 2 — Report QA

Before client delivery:

**ACCURACY → COMPLETENESS → EVIDENCE → CLARITY → ACTIONABILITY**

### Layer 3 — Periodic audit

Review a sample of completed vessel records and reports for:

- SOP adherence
- Evidence quality
- Consistency of classifications
- Follow-up discipline
- Closure verification
- Client communication quality

### Layer 4 — Management review

Look for patterns across vessels:

- Repeated missed steps
- Repeated report corrections
- Recurring vendor problems
- Unusual finding classifications
- Delayed escalations
- Capacity-related quality deterioration
- Client complaints or confusion

## Audit sample

Use a rotating sample rather than attempting to manually audit every historical record indefinitely.

Audit higher-risk or exception-heavy records more frequently.

## Defect classification

**MINOR → MATERIAL → SERIOUS → CRITICAL**

### Minor

Small process or documentation issue with limited impact.

### Material

Issue that reduces service quality or creates avoidable client confusion.

### Serious

Issue that materially affects service reliability, evidence, escalation, or client trust.

### Critical

Issue creating significant safety, security, financial, compliance, or reputational exposure.

## Corrective action

For material or greater defects:

**IDENTIFY → CONTAIN → CORRECT → VERIFY → DOCUMENT → PREVENT RECURRENCE**

## Quality scorecard

Track:

- Visit QA pass rate
- Report QA pass rate
- Rework rate
- Missing-evidence rate
- Incorrect-classification rate
- Late-report rate
- Missed-escalation rate
- Closure-verification rate
- Client-reported service defects

## Root-cause review

When a defect repeats, do not simply correct the individual record.

Ask whether the cause is:

- SOP design
- Training
- Workload
- Tooling
- Communication
- Vendor performance
- Scope ambiguity
- Scheduling

## Continuous improvement

Quality findings feed back into:

**SOPs → CHECKLISTS → TRAINING → WORKFLOW → TECHNOLOGY → MANAGEMENT CONTROLS**

## Independence protection

QA should evaluate whether MMC is maintaining its independent oversight role and avoiding unsupported technical conclusions or inappropriate vendor responsibility.

## Management rule

Growth should stop or slow when quality defects rise faster than the organization can correct them.

## Principle

MMC should scale a controlled service, not simply scale activity.
