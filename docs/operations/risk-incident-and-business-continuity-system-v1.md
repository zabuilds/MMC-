# Meridian Marine Co. — Risk, Incident & Business Continuity System v1

## Objective

Protect clients, vessels, staff, vendors, evidence, and MMC's reputation when something goes wrong.

## Incident classes

### Critical

Immediate threat to safety, major vessel damage, active security issue, major loss of essential service, or another event requiring immediate escalation.

### Urgent

Material issue requiring prompt action but not presenting an immediate critical threat.

### Operational

Routine service disruption, missed task, access issue, vendor delay, reporting problem, or system issue.

### Administrative

Minor issue that can be resolved through normal workflow.

## Universal incident flow

**DETECT → PROTECT → ASSESS → ESCALATE → COMMUNICATE → COORDINATE → VERIFY → DOCUMENT → CLOSE**

## Immediate priorities

1. Protect people and vessel where possible and appropriate.
2. Notify the appropriate emergency or responsible party when required.
3. Preserve evidence.
4. Escalate internally according to severity.
5. Communicate clearly with the client when appropriate.
6. Assign ownership and next action.

## Incident record

Record:

- Date/time
- Vessel/client
- Location
- What was observed
- Evidence available
- Severity
- Immediate actions
- Parties notified
- Owner/decision required
- Vendor/third-party involvement
- Follow-up actions
- Verification
- Closure date

## Communication principle

MMC communicates what is known, what is not known, what has been done, and what happens next.

Do not speculate or provide unsupported technical conclusions.

## Business continuity scenarios

Prepare fallback procedures for:

- Loss of primary software/system
- Loss of internet/connectivity
- Phone/communications disruption
- Staff/operator unavailability
- Vehicle/equipment failure
- Vendor failure or non-response
- Restricted vessel access
- Severe weather/storm event
- Data/evidence access problem
- Payment/billing disruption

## Manual fallback

If technology fails, service continues using a controlled manual record.

Minimum manual record:

**VESSEL → DATE/TIME → OBSERVATION → EVIDENCE → SEVERITY → ACTION → OWNER → STATUS**

Reconcile manual records into the primary system when restored.

## Vendor incident

If a vendor fails to respond or complete work:

**DETECT → CONTACT → ESCALATE → REASSIGN IF NEEDED → VERIFY → DOCUMENT**

Do not silently allow vendor failure to become an MMC service failure.

## Severe weather

Use the approved storm/emergency workflow, prioritize safety, and communicate based on verified conditions and the client's service agreement.

## Post-incident review

After material incidents:

**WHAT HAPPENED → WHY → WHAT WORKED → WHAT FAILED → CORRECTIVE ACTION → SOP UPDATE**

## Risk register

Track material risks by:

- Risk
- Likelihood
- Impact
- Existing control
- Owner
- Trigger
- Mitigation
- Review date

## Principle

MMC's reputation is built not only on preventing problems, but on responding calmly, accurately, and accountably when problems occur.
