# Meridian Marine Co. — Add-On & Expense Control Standard v1

## Purpose

Prevent scope creep and ensure owners understand when a request creates an additional charge or third-party expense.

## Add-on trigger

A request should be treated as an add-on or separate service when it is outside the active package's defined recurring scope, frequency, response level, or coordination allowance.

## Approval flow

**REQUEST → SCOPE → ESTIMATE → APPROVE → AUTHORIZE → DELIVER → RECORD**

### Request
Capture what the owner wants.

### Scope
Determine whether it is included, an add-on, or outside MMC's service model.

### Estimate
Where possible, communicate MMC's additional charge and known third-party costs before commitment.

### Approve
Obtain owner approval unless a documented emergency authority applies.

### Authorize
Record who approved and what was approved.

### Deliver
Complete only the authorized work/coordination.

### Record
Update the client/vessel record and billing record.

## Third-party expenses

Vendor invoices, fuel, parts, marina fees, specialist services, and similar costs should be clearly identified as third-party expenses unless a written agreement states otherwise.

## Expense documentation

Retain appropriate evidence such as invoice, receipt, vendor confirmation, or owner authorization.

## Scope protection

If a request repeatedly exceeds a package boundary, management should consider whether the client belongs in a higher package or Private arrangement rather than accumulating ad-hoc exceptions.
