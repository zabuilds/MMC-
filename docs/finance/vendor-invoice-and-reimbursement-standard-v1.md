# Meridian Marine Co. — Vendor Invoice & Reimbursement Standard v1

## Purpose

Keep third-party charges transparent and prevent MMC from absorbing unapproved costs.

## Vendor invoice flow

**RECEIVE → MATCH → VERIFY → APPROVE → RECORD → BILL/PAY**

### Receive
Collect the vendor invoice or service confirmation.

### Match
Match it to the correct vessel, client, work item, and authorization.

### Verify
Confirm the service was scheduled/authorized and that the charge is reasonably consistent with available information.

### Approve
Obtain the appropriate owner or MMC approval depending on whether the expense is a client pass-through or MMC operating expense.

### Record
Store the expense with the relevant operational and financial record.

### Bill/Pay
Process the charge according to the client agreement and MMC accounting process.

## Discrepancies

If an invoice cannot be matched, is materially different from the approved expectation, or appears incorrect:

- Do not represent it as final to the owner without clarification.
- Contact the vendor as appropriate.
- Record the discrepancy.
- Escalate material disputes.

## Reimbursement

MMC staff should not incur material client expenses personally unless an approved reimbursement process exists. Any authorized reimbursement should retain proof of purchase and identify the client/vessel/work item.
