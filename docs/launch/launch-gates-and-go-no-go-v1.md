# Meridian Marine Co. — Launch Gates & Go/No-Go v1

## Gate 1 — Safety

Emergency procedures, access controls, and safety boundaries are operational.

## Gate 2 — Service

MMC can reliably perform the promised package scope and deliver reports.

## Gate 3 — Coverage

Critical vendor and backup coverage exists for the launch footprint.

## Gate 4 — Client

Onboarding, communication, approval, request, and offboarding workflows are ready.

## Gate 5 — Financial

Billing, payment collection, cost tracking, and package economics controls are ready.

## Gate 6 — Technology

The minimum operational application, access controls, data integrity, auditability, and recovery procedures are reliable enough for launch.

## Gate 7 — Management

The operator can see workload, exceptions, client issues, vendor issues, and financial health without relying on hidden/manual information.

## Decision

**GO** — all critical gates sufficiently pass.

**CONDITIONAL GO** — only defined low-risk gaps remain with owners and deadlines.

**NO-GO** — any safety-critical, authorization-critical, emergency-coverage, client-trust, or core operational reliability gap remains unresolved.
