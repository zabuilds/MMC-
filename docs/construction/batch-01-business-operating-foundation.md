# Meridian Marine Co. — Construction Batch 01

## Purpose
Establish the authoritative business operating foundation before application or technology construction.

## Business Model
Meridian Marine Co. is a premium Cayman-based boat-owner oversight and concierge company. The company protects the owner's vessel, time, and peace of mind by maintaining visibility over the vessel and coordinating trusted marine services without positioning itself as a full yacht-management company.

## Core Promise
Keep the owner's vessel watched, documented, cared for, and ready.

## Operating Model
**ONBOARD → WATCH → REPORT → COORDINATE → PREPARE**

1. **Onboard** — establish the vessel profile, owner preferences, access rules, service contacts, equipment notes, and emergency instructions.
2. **Watch** — perform scheduled vessel checks and identify visible issues, risks, maintenance needs, and readiness gaps.
3. **Report** — provide clear, time-stamped findings with photographs/video where appropriate and classify issues by urgency.
4. **Coordinate** — obtain or manage the appropriate marine vendor, technician, captain, marina, fueling, detailing, repair, or other service response within the agreed authority.
5. **Prepare** — ensure agreed pre-arrival, departure, storm, service, and readiness tasks are completed and verified.

## Core Customer
Primary customer: boat owners who value their vessel but are not consistently present in Cayman to monitor it themselves.

Priority customer characteristics:
- Absentee or part-time Cayman boat owner
- Premium recreational vessel
- Limited desire to manage local vendors personally
- High value placed on reliability, documentation, and discretion
- Wants an independent advocate focused on the owner's interests

## Service Architecture

### Boat Watch — CI$225/month
Entry recurring oversight service.

Core outcome: the owner knows the vessel is being physically watched and significant visible issues are identified early.

### Boat Care — CI$395/month
Hero recurring service.

Core outcome: routine oversight plus active coordination of vessel care and maintenance needs.

### Boat Ready — CI$695/month
Premium recurring readiness service.

Core outcome: the vessel is actively maintained toward owner arrival, use, departure, and readiness requirements.

### Custom
For larger, more complex, higher-touch, or operationally unusual vessels requiring a bespoke scope.

## Supporting Services

- Scheduled vessel inspections/checks
- Photo and video documentation
- Maintenance observation and follow-up
- Vendor sourcing and coordination
- Fueling coordination
- Detailing coordination
- Pre-arrival preparation
- Departure preparation
- Storm and post-storm checks
- Emergency response coordination
- Service completion verification
- Owner-requested concierge coordination within agreed scope

## Boundaries
Meridian Marine Co. is an owner-side oversight and coordination service. Unless separately contracted and appropriately authorized, MMC does not represent itself as:

- A licensed captain or crew replacement
- A marine repair contractor
- A marina operator
- An insurer or claims adjuster
- A legal or regulatory advisor
- A substitute for required vessel management, captaincy, or crew responsibilities

Vendor work remains the responsibility of the appropriately qualified vendor. MMC's role is to observe, document, communicate, coordinate, and verify within the agreed scope.

## Customer Experience Principle
Every MMC interaction should reduce uncertainty for the owner.

The owner should consistently know:
- What was checked
- What was found
- What matters now
- What MMC recommends
- What action was taken
- What remains outstanding

## Construction Rule
The business operating model above is authoritative for Batch 01. Technology, automation, pricing expansion, and marketing materials should be built around this operating model rather than redefining it.
