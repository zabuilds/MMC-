# Meridian Marine Co. — Operator Quality Review v1

## Purpose

Create a lightweight feedback loop that improves operator consistency without turning MMC into a bureaucratic organization.

## Review areas

- Visit completeness
- Finding quality
- Evidence quality
- Severity consistency
- Escalation judgment
- Report quality
- Client communication
- Vendor coordination
- Recordkeeping
- Safety adherence

## Review rhythm

Review a representative sample of completed work on a recurring basis, with additional review after material incidents or quality exceptions.

## Coaching principle

Use reviews to improve judgment, consistency, and process adherence. Corrective feedback should identify the expected standard and the practical change required.

## Training triggers

Repeated gaps may trigger:

- Refresher training
- SOP clarification
- Checklist change
- System control
- Vendor process change
- Management review
