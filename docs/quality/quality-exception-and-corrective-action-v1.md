# Meridian Marine Co. — Quality Exception & Corrective Action v1

## Purpose

Turn service failures into controlled improvements rather than recurring problems.

## Quality exception triggers

- Missed or materially incomplete visit
- Incorrect or incomplete report
- Missed escalation
- Unauthorized scope/spending
- Repeated vendor failure
- Client complaint
- Security/access mistake
- Material record discrepancy
- Repeated overdue action

## Corrective workflow

**IDENTIFY → CONTAIN → ASSESS → CORRECT → VERIFY → LEARN → CLOSE**

### Identify
Record what happened, affected client/vessel, date, and evidence.

### Contain
Take immediate steps to protect the client, vessel, or relationship.

### Assess
Determine cause, impact, recurrence risk, and whether escalation is required.

### Correct
Fix the immediate problem and assign responsibility.

### Verify
Confirm the correction actually worked.

### Learn
Determine whether SOPs, training, vendor selection, system controls, or communication should change.

### Close
Record the final outcome and close only when required corrective actions are complete.

## Management rule

Repeated exceptions should trigger management review and potentially a process redesign rather than repeated one-off fixes.
