# Meridian Marine Co. — Service Quality Assurance Standard v1

## Quality objective

Every client interaction and vessel visit should be consistent, factual, timely, and traceable.

## Visit quality checks

Before closeout, verify:

- Correct vessel/client
- Required checklist completed or limitation recorded
- Material observations documented
- Evidence attached where appropriate
- Severity assigned correctly
- Actions created where needed
- Owner approvals identified
- Security/access restored
- Report status recorded

## Report quality checks

Reports must be:

- Factual
- Clear
- Complete within scope
- Free of unsupported diagnosis
- Clear about owner decisions
- Clear about unresolved matters

## Vendor quality checks

Track:

- Scope clarity
- Authorization
- Arrival/completion timing
- Material scope changes
- Evidence of completion
- Outstanding issues
- Cost discrepancies

## Client experience checks

Monitor:

- Response timeliness
- Communication clarity
- Missed commitments
- Repeated questions caused by poor records
- Complaints and escalations

## Quality principle

Quality assurance is part of normal operations, not a separate activity performed only after something goes wrong.
