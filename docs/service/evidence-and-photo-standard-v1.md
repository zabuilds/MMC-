# Meridian Marine Co. — Evidence & Photo Standard v1

## Purpose

Create useful, consistent evidence without overwhelming the owner with unnecessary media.

## Evidence principles

Evidence should be:

- Relevant
- Clear
- Time-associated
- Location-specific where useful
- Sufficient to support the observation
- Stored with the correct visit/finding

## Photo priorities

Capture images when they materially help:

- Establish normal baseline condition
- Show a defect or change
- Show water intrusion/damage
- Support a safety/security concern
- Document vendor work/completion
- Demonstrate readiness status
- Explain an owner decision

## Avoid

- Excessive duplicate photos
- Photos with no operational value
- Unnecessary sensitive information
- Images that imply specialist diagnosis
- Unclear photos that cannot support the finding

## Finding evidence

Material findings should normally have supporting evidence where safe and practical. If evidence cannot be captured, record why.

## Privacy & security

Sensitive access information, credentials, alarm codes, keys, or unrelated personal information should not be unnecessarily captured or included in ordinary report media.

## Evidence integrity

Do not edit evidence in a way that changes the meaning of the observation. Any derived annotation should remain distinguishable from the original evidence.
