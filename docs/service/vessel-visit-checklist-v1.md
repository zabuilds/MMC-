# Meridian Marine Co. — Vessel Visit Checklist v1

## 1. Pre-visit

- Confirm client and vessel
- Confirm scheduled visit and access
- Review previous visit/report
- Review open findings and actions
- Review special owner instructions
- Review known vendor activity
- Confirm required equipment/evidence method

## 2. Arrival & access

- Confirm correct vessel/location
- Record arrival time
- Observe access/security condition
- Confirm authorized access
- Check obvious external condition before entry

## 3. Exterior / visible condition

- Hull/visible exterior condition
- Decks and walking surfaces
- Lines/fenders where visible
- Covers/hatches
- Visible signs of damage
- Visible water intrusion
- Obvious corrosion or deterioration

## 4. Interior / accessible spaces

- General condition
- Unusual odors
- Visible leaks/moisture
- Bilge/low-point condition where safely accessible
- Electrical/utility indicators where safely observable
- HVAC/environmental indicators where applicable
- Signs of pests or intrusion
- Unusual sounds or conditions

## 5. Systems & readiness observations

Only observe items within MMC's approved oversight scope.

- Shore power/utility connection condition where visible
- Battery/monitoring indicators where provided and safely observable
- Water systems indicators where visible
- Pumps/alarms/indicators where safely observable
- Refrigeration/freezer condition where applicable
- Security/access systems where relevant
- Readiness items for upcoming owner use

## 6. Findings

For each material finding:

- Describe what was observed
- Record exact location
- Capture evidence where appropriate
- Assign severity
- Identify immediate action if required
- Identify whether owner approval is needed

## 7. Closeout

- Confirm no required area was missed or record limitation
- Restore access/security
- Record departure time
- Update actions
- Escalate Critical/Urgent matters
- Prepare report
- Schedule required follow-up

## Safety boundary

MMC does not enter unsafe areas, perform specialist diagnosis, operate equipment outside approved scope, or create a false impression that a vessel has been technically inspected beyond the actual service performed.
