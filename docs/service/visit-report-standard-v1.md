# Meridian Marine Co. — Vessel Visit Report Standard v1

## Purpose

Give owners a consistent, useful record of what MMC observed and what happens next.

## Report structure

### Header

- Client
- Vessel
- Visit date/time
- Operator
- Visit type
- Completion status

### Executive summary

A concise plain-language summary of the vessel's observed condition and any material matters.

### Findings

For each material finding:

- Finding title
- Severity
- What was observed
- Evidence reference
- Why it matters, where supportable
- Recommended/required next action
- Owner approval requirement
- Current status

### Actions

Show open and completed actions with responsibility, due date, and status.

### Vendor activity

Identify relevant vendor coordination, scheduled work, completion information, and unresolved matters.

### Readiness/upcoming use

Where applicable, identify known readiness needs or upcoming commitments.

### Limitations

Clearly state anything that could not be checked, verified, accessed, or completed.

### Next steps

State what MMC will do next, what the owner must decide, and what is awaiting a vendor or external party.

## Writing standard

Reports should be calm, factual, concise, evidence-based, and understandable to an owner who was not present.

Avoid unsupported diagnosis, exaggerated language, unnecessary jargon, and false certainty.
