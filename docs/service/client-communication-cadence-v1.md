# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Communication Cadence v1

## Principle

Communicate enough to create confidence and visibility without creating noise.

## Routine

Recurring service should provide a predictable report cadence aligned to the package and visit schedule.

## Event-driven

Communicate outside the routine cadence when there is:

- Critical or Urgent finding
- Material security/access issue
- Material vendor issue
- Significant readiness concern
- Owner approval required
- Material change affecting an expected outcome

## Communication hierarchy

1. Immediate safety/emergency communication
2. Time-sensitive owner decision
3. Material operational update
4. Routine report
5. Administrative communication

## Communication quality

Messages should state:

**WHAT HAPPENED → WHY IT MATTERS → WHAT MMC DID → WHAT IS NEEDED → WHAT HAPPENS NEXT**

Avoid unsupported diagnosis, unnecessary alarm, vague requests, or technical detail that does not help the owner make a decision.
