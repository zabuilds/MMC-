# Meridian Marine Co. — Material Finding Follow-Up Standard v1

## Purpose

Ensure important observations do not disappear after a report is delivered.

## Follow-up record

Each material finding should identify:

- Finding
- Severity
- Vessel
- Owner/client
- Responsible party
- Required action
- Approval requirement
- Due date
- Current status
- Evidence
- Verification requirement

## Follow-up cadence

Follow-up frequency should reflect severity, risk, owner expectations, and the agreed service scope.

Critical and Urgent matters receive active escalation until responsibility is confirmed and the immediate risk is controlled.

Action Required matters remain in the operational queue until completed or formally reclassified.

## Closure

A finding is not closed merely because a vendor says work is complete. Where appropriate, MMC should verify completion through evidence or a follow-up observation.

## Reclassification

Conditions may be reclassified when new evidence changes the understanding of the issue. Material reclassification should preserve the prior state and reason for change.
