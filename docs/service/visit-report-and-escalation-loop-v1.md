# Meridian Marine Co. — Visit Report & Escalation Loop v1

## Report flow

**VISIT COMPLETE → REVIEW → ESCALATE → DRAFT → QA → DELIVER → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE**

## Report structure

1. Visit summary
2. Vessel condition overview
3. What was observed
4. Material findings by severity
5. Evidence
6. Actions required
7. Owner approvals required
8. Vendor activity/status
9. Readiness status
10. Limitations or inaccessible areas
11. Recommended next steps

## Escalation timing

### Critical
Immediate escalation through the approved emergency communication path. Do not wait for routine report delivery.

### Urgent
Escalate promptly and confirm receipt where appropriate.

### Action Required
Include in the report and create a tracked action with responsible party and due date.

### Monitor
Record the condition and compare during subsequent visits.

### Informational
Record for owner visibility where useful.

## Follow-up loop

Every material finding must reach one of these states:

**ACTIONED → VERIFIED → CLOSED**

or

**MONITORED → REVIEWED → CLOSED/CONTINUED**

## Owner communication principle

Communications should distinguish clearly between what MMC observed, what MMC recommends, what requires specialist input, and what decision belongs to the owner.
