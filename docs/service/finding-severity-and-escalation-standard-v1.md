# Meridian Marine Co. — Finding Severity & Escalation Standard v1

## Severity levels

### Critical
Immediate or potentially severe threat to safety, security, vessel/property integrity, or major loss. Escalate immediately.

### Urgent
Material issue requiring prompt attention to prevent deterioration, disruption, or meaningful loss. Escalate promptly.

### Action Required
Issue should be addressed within an appropriate planned timeframe.

### Monitor
Condition should remain visible and be reassessed during future oversight.

### Informational
Relevant observation that does not currently require action.

## Escalation rule

Severity should reflect the observed condition and available evidence, not speculation.

When uncertain between two levels, document the uncertainty and escalate when the potential consequence is material.

## Owner decision

Where action requires owner approval, clearly separate:

- What MMC observed
- Why it matters
- What action is recommended
- What approval is required
- What happens if action is delayed

## Specialist boundary

MMC may identify and document visible conditions and coordinate qualified specialists. MMC should not provide unsupported specialist diagnosis.
