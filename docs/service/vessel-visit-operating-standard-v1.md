# Meridian Marine Co. — Vessel Visit Operating Standard v1

## Purpose

Define the repeatable field process MMC uses to deliver vessel oversight consistently.

## Operating cycle

**PREPARE → ARRIVE → SECURE → INSPECT → DOCUMENT → ASSESS → ESCALATE → REPORT → FOLLOW UP → CLOSE**

## 1. Prepare

Review the vessel profile, previous visit, open findings/actions, owner instructions, access requirements, upcoming arrival/departure information, and any known concerns.

Confirm the visit scope and required equipment before departure.

## 2. Arrive

Confirm correct vessel and location. Record arrival time and note any access or safety limitation before beginning the inspection.

## 3. Secure

Follow the approved access/security procedure. Protect keys, access codes, and other sensitive information. Do not expose sensitive access information in ordinary notes or communications.

## 4. Inspect

Perform the applicable package/checklist scope systematically. Observe visible conditions relevant to the agreed oversight service. Do not represent MMC as performing specialist diagnostics unless specifically qualified and authorized to do so.

## 5. Document

Record material observations with clear descriptions and appropriate photo/video evidence. Distinguish direct observation from assumption or third-party information.

## 6. Assess

Classify findings using the established severity framework. Identify whether the matter requires monitoring, action, owner approval, vendor coordination, or immediate escalation.

## 7. Escalate

Critical and Urgent matters bypass routine reporting cadence. Notify the appropriate owner/contact promptly and record what was communicated and what action was taken.

## 8. Report

Produce the visit report with:

- Visit details
- Scope/completion status
- Material observations
- Evidence
- Finding severity
- Actions
- Owner decisions required
- Vendor coordination required
- Unresolved limitations
- Next steps

## 9. Follow up

Create or update operational actions. Track owner approvals, vendor commitments, due dates, and unresolved matters.

## 10. Close

Confirm the vessel is left in the appropriate state, access/security procedures are complete, records are saved, and the visit status accurately reflects what occurred.

## Quality rule

A visit is never marked fully complete merely because the operator arrived. Completion reflects the actual scope performed and any material limitations recorded.
