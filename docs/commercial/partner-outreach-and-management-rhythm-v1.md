# Meridian Marine Co. — Partner Outreach & Management Rhythm v1

## Purpose

Turn the partnership strategy into a repeatable relationship-building process.

## Weekly activity

- Identify qualified potential partners
- Make targeted introductions
- Follow up on active conversations
- Record outcomes
- Schedule next relationship action

## Monthly activity

- Review active partner relationships
- Review introductions and conversion
- Identify high-value partner categories
- Identify weak or inactive relationships
- Share useful MMC service information where appropriate

## Outreach standard

Keep outreach concise, credible, and owner-focused.

Lead with the problem MMC solves:

**Owners are often away from their vessels and need someone local, independent, and accountable to watch what they own.**

## Partner conversation

Explore:

- Who the partner serves
- What owner problems they see repeatedly
- Where clients lack local oversight
- What happens when a vessel issue is discovered
- Where vendor coordination breaks down
- Which owners are strongest fits for MMC

## Follow-up outcomes

**ACTIVE → NURTURE → NOT A FIT → PARTNERED**

## Relationship principle

Do not chase every organization. Build a smaller network of trusted relationships that consistently produce good-fit introductions and strengthen client service.
