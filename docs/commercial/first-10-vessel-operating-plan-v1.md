# Meridian Marine Co. — First 10 Vessel Operating Plan v1

## Objective

Move from the first successful pilot vessel to the first ten active vessels without sacrificing service quality or losing the learning discipline established during the pilot.

## Growth sequence

### Vessels 1–3 — Prove

- Validate the full service cycle
- Capture actual delivery time
- Identify recurring exceptions
- Refine client communication
- Confirm package fit

### Vessels 4–6 — Stabilize

- Standardize recurring visit preparation
- Tighten reporting and QA
- Improve scheduling efficiency
- Build vendor redundancy
- Identify workload bottlenecks

### Vessels 7–10 — Repeat

- Operate the standardized workflow consistently
- Begin delegating repeatable field/admin work where justified
- Protect response and reporting standards
- Track package economics by vessel
- Strengthen referral and partner acquisition

## Weekly management rhythm

Review:

- Active vessels
- New prospects
- Discovery conversations
- Proposals
- Scheduled visits
- Critical/Urgent findings
- Open actions
- Reports due
- Vendor exceptions
- Capacity utilization
- Client concerns

## Vessel growth gate

Do not add volume automatically. Before each growth step confirm:

**SERVICE QUALITY + CAPACITY + CLIENT EXPERIENCE + ECONOMICS + OPERATIONAL CONTROL**

## First-10 KPIs

- Active vessels
- New vessels added
- Package mix
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Contribution by vessel
- Visit completion rate
- Report turnaround
- Open/overdue actions
- Critical/Urgent response performance
- Client retention
- Referral-sourced vessels
- Partner-sourced vessels

## Founder focus

During the first ten vessels, the founder should spend increasing time on:

**CUSTOMER TRUST + SALES + QUALITY + SYSTEM IMPROVEMENT**

rather than personally performing every repeatable administrative task.

## Graduation point

At ten active vessels, conduct a formal review of:

- Capacity
- Staffing needs
- Package economics
- Service quality
- Retention
- Referral performance
- Vendor network
- Technology gaps

Then determine whether MMC is ready to move into the 11–25 vessel growth stage.

## Principle

The first ten vessels are the bridge between a validated service and a scalable operating company.
