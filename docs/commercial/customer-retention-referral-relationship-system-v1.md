# Meridian Marine Co. — Customer Retention, Referral & Relationship System v1

## Objective

Turn reliable service delivery into long-term recurring relationships, referrals, and stronger client trust.

## Relationship lifecycle

**ONBOARD → DELIVER → COMMUNICATE → REVIEW → RENEW → REFER → EXPAND**

## Retention foundation

Retention is earned through consistent delivery rather than retention campaigns alone.

Protect:

- Reliable visit completion
- Clear reporting
- Prompt escalation
- Follow-through on open items
- Accurate records
- Consistent communication
- Visible evidence of value

## Client health signals

### Healthy

Client receives service consistently, communicates normally, and shows confidence in MMC.

### Watch

Reduced engagement, repeated questions, unresolved concerns, service friction, or declining perceived value.

### At risk

Material complaint, repeated service failure, payment friction, explicit dissatisfaction, or credible cancellation signal.

## Client review rhythm

At appropriate intervals review:

- What MMC has observed
- Actions completed
- Recurring issues
- Vendor activity
- Upcoming watch points
- Whether current service level still fits the vessel

Do not manufacture upgrade opportunities. Recommend changes only when the client's actual need supports them.

## Renewal principle

A renewal conversation should make the value delivered visible:

**WHAT WE WATCHED → WHAT WE IDENTIFIED → WHAT WE COORDINATED → WHAT WE VERIFIED → WHAT THE OWNER NOW KNOWS**

## Referral system

Ask for referrals after a clear value moment, such as:

- Successful issue identification
- Useful vendor coordination
- Storm/event support
- Consistent service milestone
- Positive client feedback

Referral request should be simple and optional.

## Referral quality

Prioritize referrals that resemble MMC's best-fit client profile rather than maximizing referral volume.

## Relationship expansion

Expansion may include additional service cadence or vessel coverage only when justified by:

**CLIENT NEED + VESSEL NEED + CAPACITY + ECONOMICS**

## Recovery workflow

When a client is dissatisfied:

**LISTEN → ACKNOWLEDGE → INVESTIGATE → CORRECT → VERIFY → FOLLOW UP → LEARN**

Do not become defensive or shift blame to vendors before the facts are established.

## Customer KPIs

Track:

- Retention rate
- Churn rate
- At-risk clients
- Referral rate
- Referral conversion
- Expansion revenue
- Client issues
- Recovery outcomes

## Relationship principle

MMC should become the trusted local presence an owner is reluctant to lose because the service reduces uncertainty and consistently follows through.
