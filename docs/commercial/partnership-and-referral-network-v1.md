# Meridian Marine Co. — Partnership & Referral Network v1

## Objective

Build a trusted network of professionals who can introduce qualified vessel owners to MMC and strengthen service delivery without compromising MMC's independent oversight position.

## Priority partner categories

### Tier 1 — Highest priority

- Yacht brokers
- Marina operators/management
- Marine service companies
- Yacht management professionals
- Private client/concierge professionals

### Tier 2 — Strategic

- Insurance professionals
- Attorneys and fiduciary professionals
- Relocation/advisory professionals
- High-end hospitality/concierge relationships
- Other trusted professionals serving vessel owners

### Tier 3 — Community/network

- Yacht clubs
- Owner communities
- Events and industry groups
- Relevant local business networks

## Partner value proposition

MMC gives partners a credible independent oversight resource for owners who:

- Are away from their vessel
- Need reliable local visibility
- Need vendor coordination without hiring a full manager
- Want better readiness before arrival
- Need an independent set of eyes

## Partner qualification

Prioritize partners with:

- Strong owner relationships
- Reputation for trust and professionalism
- Relevant Cayman market access
- Clear service boundaries
- Willingness to introduce MMC when appropriate

## Partnership model

The initial model should emphasize:

**TRUST → INTRODUCTION → QUALIFICATION → SERVICE → FEEDBACK**

Do not make referral payments or promises until the commercial and legal framework for such arrangements has been separately approved.

## Partner onboarding

Record:

- Organization/contact
- Category
- Relationship owner
- Client profile served
- Introduction potential
- Last interaction
- Next action
- Referral history
- Quality/fit notes

## Partner development rhythm

Each month:

- Add qualified relationships
- Deepen existing relationships
- Provide useful service education
- Share appropriate MMC updates
- Ask for introductions where genuinely relevant
- Review referral quality

## Referral handoff

**PARTNER INTRODUCTION → MMC QUALIFICATION → DISCOVERY → PROPOSAL → CLOSE → ONBOARD → FEEDBACK TO PARTNER**

The partner should never be left wondering what happened to an introduction, subject to appropriate client confidentiality.

## Protection of brand position

MMC should be presented as an independent oversight and confidence service—not as a contractor, marina substitute, or general property manager.

## KPIs

Track:

- Active partners
- Qualified introductions
- Introduction-to-discovery rate
- Discovery-to-client conversion
- Revenue from partner-sourced clients
- Partner retention
- Referral quality

## Principle

The best MMC partnerships are built around mutual trust and client value, not volume for its own sake.
