# Meridian Marine Co. — Partner & Referral Channel System v1

## Objective

Build a repeatable network of trusted referral sources that can introduce MMC to qualified vessel owners without compromising independence or client trust.

## Priority partner groups

1. Yacht/marine brokers
2. Marinas and dock operators
3. Marine service professionals
4. Yacht management contacts where roles do not conflict
5. Insurance and finance professionals where appropriate
6. Concierge/luxury service providers
7. Existing clients and their trusted networks

## Ideal partner profile

Prioritize partners who:

- Regularly interact with vessel owners
- Understand owner pain points
- Have strong local credibility
- Serve absentee or high-value owners
- Communicate professionally
- Respect MMC's independence
- Can identify a genuine service fit

## Partner value proposition

MMC should make the partner's job easier, not ask them to sell the service.

Core message:

**When an owner needs an independent local set of eyes on their vessel, MMC provides the oversight, documentation, communication, and coordination.**

## Introduction flow

**IDENTIFY FIT → INTRODUCE MMC → CONSENT/CONNECTION → DISCOVERY → QUALIFY → PROPOSE → CLOSE → THANK PARTNER**

## Partner onboarding

Record:

- Partner identity
- Organization
- Role
- Contact method
- Relationship strength
- Client profile served
- Potential fit
- Conflicts/limitations
- Introduction history
- Referral outcomes

## Partner relationship rhythm

Maintain relationships through useful, professional contact rather than constant sales requests.

Possible touchpoints:

- Introduction
- Successful referral update where appropriate
- Service capability update
- Useful local marine information
- Periodic relationship check-in

## Referral handling

When a partner introduces an owner:

- Respond promptly
- Thank the partner
- Protect the owner's privacy
- Treat the prospect independently
- Do not promise an outcome before qualification
- Record source attribution

## Independence boundary

Partner relationships must never override the owner's interests, distort reporting, or create pressure to recommend unnecessary work.

## Referral economics

Track referral source and outcome. Any future referral incentive or commercial arrangement must be separately reviewed for business, contractual, tax, and legal implications before implementation.

## Channel KPIs

- Active partners
- Qualified introductions
- Discovery conversations from partners
- Proposals from partner leads
- Closed vessels from partners
- Recurring revenue from partner channel
- Partner conversion rate
- Referral quality
- Partner retention/engagement

## Channel development sequence

**FOUNDATION → FIRST PARTNERS → TEST → MEASURE → REFINE → REPEAT → SCALE**

Do not build a large partner network before proving which partner types actually produce qualified clients.

## Principle

The best partner channel is not the largest network. It is a small group of trusted professionals who naturally encounter the exact owners MMC is designed to help.
