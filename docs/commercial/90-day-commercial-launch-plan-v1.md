# Meridian Marine Co. — 90-Day Commercial Launch Plan v1

## Objective

Convert MMC from a prepared operating concept into a functioning recurring-revenue service with disciplined customer acquisition, delivery, measurement, and learning.

## Days 1–30 — Launch foundation

### Commercial

- Finalize the practical sales offer using the approved package baseline
- Build the first qualified prospect list
- Begin direct outreach and discovery conversations
- Begin partner introductions
- Track every opportunity from first contact through outcome

### Service

- Prepare onboarding materials
- Prepare vessel baseline process
- Prepare visit checklist
- Prepare report format
- Prepare incident/escalation workflow
- Prepare vendor contact network

### Management

- Start weekly KPI review
- Start weekly cash review
- Record actual service time from the first delivery
- Record all material operational exceptions

## Days 31–60 — Prove repeatability

### Commercial

- Continue qualified prospecting
- Follow up systematically on open opportunities
- Test partner channel performance
- Collect client feedback after meaningful service moments
- Identify strongest acquisition sources

### Service

- Deliver recurring visits consistently
- Compare findings against vessel baselines
- Track report turnaround
- Track open/overdue actions
- Measure vendor response and completion
- Perform periodic QA audits

### Management

- Review package economics using actual workload
- Review capacity utilization
- Identify repeatable tasks suitable for delegation
- Correct recurring process defects

## Days 61–90 — Build the operating engine

### Commercial

- Double down on the highest-quality acquisition channels
- Strengthen referrals from satisfied clients
- Develop priority partner relationships
- Improve proposal and follow-up conversion
- Review customer retention signals

### Service

- Standardize the highest-frequency workflows
- Strengthen vendor redundancy
- Tighten QA
- Improve reporting efficiency without reducing evidence quality
- Formalize lessons learned into SOP updates

### Management

- Conduct 90-day business review
- Review active vessels and recurring revenue
- Review contribution and capacity by package/vessel complexity
- Review retention and client health
- Identify the largest operational bottlenecks
- Decide whether additional capacity is justified

## Weekly founder rhythm

### Monday

**PIPELINE + PRIORITIES + CAPACITY**

### Midweek

**CLIENTS + SERVICE EXCEPTIONS + VENDORS**

### Friday

**DELIVERY + CASH + KPIs + LESSONS**

## 90-day success indicators

The launch is progressing when MMC demonstrates:

- Paying recurring vessels
- Reliable scheduled service
- Consistent reporting
- Controlled incident response
- Repeatable vendor coordination
- Positive client confidence signals
- Measurable referral activity
- Understandable package economics
- Known capacity limits
- A documented operating system that another person can learn

## What not to optimize yet

Do not prioritize:

- Large fixed overhead
- Broad geographic expansion
- Complex hiring structures
- Excessive technology customization
- Large marketing spend before channel evidence
- Too many service variations

## 90-day decision gate

At day 90 determine whether MMC should:

**CONTINUE PILOT → STABILIZE → ADD CAPACITY → EXPAND SALES → ENTER 11–25 VESSEL STAGE**

The decision should be based on evidence, not excitement.

## Principle

The first 90 days are not about looking like a large company. They are about proving that MMC can consistently acquire, serve, retain, and economically support real vessel owners.
