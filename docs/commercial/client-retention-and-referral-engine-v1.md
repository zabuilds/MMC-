# Meridian Marine Co. — Client Retention & Referral Engine v1

## Objective

Turn reliable service delivery into long-term recurring relationships and qualified referrals without relying on discounts or aggressive selling.

## Retention lifecycle

**ACTIVATE → DELIVER → COMMUNICATE → REVIEW → IMPROVE → RENEW → REFER**

## Client confidence moments

Prioritize communication after meaningful events:

- First vessel activation
- First baseline report
- Critical/Urgent finding resolution
- Significant vendor completion
- Major readiness milestone
- Meaningful service improvement
- Regular service review

## Service review

Periodically confirm:

- Service is meeting expectations
- Package remains appropriate
- Vessel circumstances have changed
- Communication preferences remain correct
- Outstanding issues are understood
- Additional requirements should be considered

## Churn-risk signals

Watch for:

- Repeated dissatisfaction
- Unresolved service issues
- Communication delays
- Frequent scope disputes
- Declining engagement
- Repeated billing friction
- Service no longer matching vessel/owner needs

## Retention response

**DETECT → UNDERSTAND → CORRECT → CONFIRM → DOCUMENT**

Do not automatically discount. First determine whether the issue is service quality, communication, scope, package fit, or price/value perception.

## Referral engine

Ask for referrals after a clear value moment, not immediately after every interaction.

Best moments include:

- Successful resolution of a meaningful issue
- Strong first report
- Positive service review
- Client explicitly expresses confidence or satisfaction

## Referral principle

The referral request should feel like a natural extension of a trusted relationship, not a sales demand.

## Referral tracking

Track:

- Referring client
- Referral date
- Prospect source
- Prospect status
- Conversion outcome
- Resulting recurring revenue

## Growth loop

**GOOD SERVICE → CLIENT CONFIDENCE → RETENTION → REFERRAL → QUALIFIED PROSPECT → NEW CLIENT**

## Management KPIs

- Retention rate
- Churn rate
- Referral rate
- Referral-to-client conversion
- Revenue from referrals
- Client service issues
- Resolution time
- Client satisfaction/confidence signals

## Principle

MMC should earn retention and referrals through dependable independent oversight, not incentives that weaken premium positioning.
