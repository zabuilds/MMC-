# Meridian Marine Co. — Capacity Trigger & Staffing Framework v1

## Purpose

Define when MMC should add capacity before service quality is compromised.

## Capacity review signals

Review capacity when two or more of the following persist:

- Recurring visit backlog
- Report turnaround slippage
- Rising overdue actions
- Excessive operator workload
- Increasing travel inefficiency
- Growing vendor coordination burden
- Reduced management response time
- Client communication delays

## Staffing sequence

**FOUNDER-LED → FIELD SUPPORT → OPERATIONS SUPPORT → FIELD TEAM → MANAGEMENT LAYER**

The exact timing should be driven by measured workload and service standards rather than arbitrary vessel counts.

## Delegation principle

Delegate repeatable execution before delegating relationship ownership or critical decision authority.

## Quality protection

New capacity should be added early enough to allow training, shadowing, QA, and transition before the existing team reaches failure capacity.

## Management review

Capacity should be reviewed at least monthly during early growth and more frequently when workload or service exceptions rise sharply.
