# Meridian Marine Co. — Financial & Commercial Operations v1

## Purpose

Protect recurring revenue, control service costs, preserve approval discipline, and make package economics visible.

## Revenue model

MMC recurring packages are the primary predictable revenue layer. Additional authorized services, vendor coordination, and custom requirements should remain distinguishable from recurring package revenue.

## Commercial controls

Every billable item should have:

- Client/vessel
- Service or package relationship
- Description
- Authorization basis
- Amount
- Status
- Supporting record
- Billing period or date

## Vendor cost control

Third-party costs should be linked to the relevant client, vessel, request, action, or vendor job where practical.

Material cost changes require review against the approved scope and authorization.

## Package economics

Management should monitor:

- Revenue per vessel
- Expected service load
- Direct vendor/service costs
- Gross margin
- Unplanned workload
- Package utilization
- Upgrade/downgrade patterns

## Commercial principle

MMC should not grow revenue by quietly expanding scope. Scope expansion should be explicit, authorized, and commercially visible.
