# Meridian Marine Co. — Pricing Review Framework v1

## Current package prices

- Harbour — CI$225/month
- Steward — CI$395/month
- Reserve — CI$675/month
- Custom — scope-dependent

These are the current authoritative construction prices.

## Review principle

Pricing should be reviewed from evidence, not instinct alone.

Evaluate:

- Customer willingness to pay
- Perceived value
- Actual service burden
- Travel burden
- Reporting burden
- Vendor coordination burden
- Capacity consumption
- Contribution margin
- Retention
- Upgrade/downgrade behavior

## Early-stage rule

Do not repeatedly change public pricing during the initial validation period unless evidence shows a material problem.

Instead, gather real objections and buying behavior.

## Pricing signals

### Possible underpricing

- Strong close rate with minimal price resistance
- High demand for higher-touch service
- Service burden materially exceeds price assumptions
- Reserve demand indicates willingness to pay for greater readiness

### Possible overpricing

- Consistent qualified objections specifically tied to price
- Strong service interest but repeated loss at proposal stage
- Competitor alternatives materially outperform MMC on comparable scope/value

## Adjustment hierarchy

Before reducing a price:

1. Clarify value
2. Clarify scope
3. Improve sales qualification
4. Confirm service burden
5. Test package configuration
6. Only then consider price adjustment

## Goal

Maintain premium positioning while ensuring each package is economically sustainable and genuinely valuable to the client.
