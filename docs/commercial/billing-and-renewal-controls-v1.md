# Meridian Marine Co. — Billing & Renewal Controls v1

## Billing lifecycle

**READY TO BILL → INVOICED → PAYMENT PENDING → PAID → OVERDUE → RESOLVED**

## Billing controls

Before billing, confirm:

- Active agreement
- Correct client/vessel
- Correct package/service period
- Approved additional charges where applicable
- Supporting operational records
- No known billing dispute

## Overdue handling

Overdue balances should follow a controlled escalation process that distinguishes administrative reminders from service-impacting decisions.

## Renewal review

Before renewal or annual commercial review, evaluate:

- Current package
- Actual service load
- Open issues
- Client satisfaction signals
- Pricing/margin performance
- Upcoming vessel needs
- Requested changes

## Renewal outcomes

- Renew unchanged
- Renew with revised scope
- Upgrade
- Downgrade
- Move to custom
- Do not renew

## Commercial boundary

No renewal change should silently alter operational scope. Commercial and service changes should remain explicit and documented.
