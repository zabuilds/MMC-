# Meridian Marine Co. — Batch 1 Service Architecture

## Commercial foundation

MMC provides premium independent vessel oversight and concierge support for boat and yacht owners who want confidence that their vessel is being watched, documented, coordinated, and prepared without engaging MMC as captain, yacht manager, cleaner, contractor, or repair provider.

## Operating model

**ONBOARD → WATCH → REPORT → COORDINATE → PREPARE**

### Onboard
Establish the vessel profile, owner preferences, access requirements, emergency contacts, service history, vendor relationships, and recurring operating needs.

### Watch
Conduct scheduled vessel checks against a defined inspection standard appropriate to the package and vessel.

### Report
Document observations with clear written findings and supporting photos/video where appropriate. Escalate material issues promptly.

### Coordinate
With owner approval and within MMC's authority, coordinate qualified third-party vendors, service providers, captains, marinas, and other relevant parties.

### Prepare
Support owner arrival/departure readiness, planned service coordination, fueling/service preparation, and other agreed pre-arrival requirements.

## Locked packages

### Harbour — CI$225/month
Purpose: dependable baseline oversight.

Core outcome: the owner knows the vessel is being checked and material issues are surfaced.

### Steward — CI$395/month
Purpose: comprehensive recurring vessel care and coordination.

Core outcome: MMC becomes the owner's ongoing operational oversight layer for routine vessel needs.

### Reserve — CI$675/month
Purpose: highest standard recurring oversight, readiness, priority support, and deeper coordination.

Core outcome: the vessel receives the highest standard level of recurring attention available under MMC's standard packages.

### Private — Custom
Purpose: bespoke requirements for complex vessels, owners, schedules, or operating arrangements.

Core outcome: a tailored operating arrangement designed around the vessel and owner's requirements.

## Package design rules

1. Each tier must have a clearly distinguishable customer outcome.
2. Higher tiers increase frequency, responsiveness, coordination depth, readiness support, and/or service scope—not merely the number of reports.
3. Emergency response must remain operationally defined and must not imply unlimited availability unless separately contracted.
4. Vendor work remains third-party work; MMC coordinates and verifies rather than performing regulated, licensed, or specialized repairs unless separately authorized and qualified.
5. Every package must preserve clear boundaries between oversight, coordination, and actual vessel management/captaincy.
6. Additional work outside package scope must be explicitly identified before it becomes recurring operational responsibility.
7. Package prices are launch prices and remain subject to review after real operating data is collected.
