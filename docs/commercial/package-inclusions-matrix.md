# Meridian Marine Co. — Package Inclusions Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix defines the commercial boundary between MMC's standard recurring packages. It is an operating baseline, not permission to perform regulated, licensed, mechanical, electrical, or repair work outside MMC's qualifications.

## Harbour

### Customer promise
Reliable baseline oversight that gives an owner confidence the vessel is being checked and important issues are surfaced.

### Standard service boundary
- One scheduled recurring vessel check per month.
- Standard visual condition review.
- Check of agreed accessible areas and observable systems/conditions.
- Photo documentation of notable findings.
- Concise owner report after the visit.
- Prompt escalation of material concerns.
- Basic coordination of an identified issue where reasonably necessary, with additional work outside the package treated as an add-on or separately authorized service.

### Harbour is intentionally limited
Harbour is not designed to absorb ongoing maintenance administration, frequent owner requests, extensive vendor management, or routine pre-arrival preparation.

## Steward

### Customer promise
An ongoing operational oversight layer that keeps the vessel monitored while helping the owner coordinate routine care.

### Standard service boundary
- Two scheduled recurring vessel checks per month.
- Enhanced condition documentation and reporting.
- Maintenance/service coordination for routine needs.
- Vendor communication and appointment coordination.
- Follow-up tracking on identified maintenance items.
- Routine pre-arrival and departure readiness coordination.
- Fueling/service appointment coordination.
- Storm/event readiness support within defined scope.
- Defined emergency coordination support.
- Reasonable recurring owner requests related to the vessel's oversight.

### Steward is the hero package
Steward should deliver the strongest perceived value without becoming an unlimited concierge arrangement.

## Reserve

### Customer promise
The highest standard MMC recurring service: deeper oversight, priority coordination, readiness support, and faster operational response.

### Standard service boundary
- Weekly scheduled vessel oversight, subject to vessel location/access and agreed operating calendar.
- Premium condition documentation and reporting.
- Priority maintenance/service coordination.
- Deeper vendor coordination and follow-up.
- Pre-arrival and departure readiness coordination.
- Fueling/service preparation coordination.
- Priority storm/event readiness support.
- Priority emergency coordination within defined scope.
- Priority scheduling and owner-response handling.
- Expanded maintenance issue tracking and status visibility.
- Additional recurring oversight tasks agreed during onboarding where operationally reasonable.

### Reserve is not unlimited
Reserve does not mean unlimited visits, unlimited emergency labor, captaincy, yacht management, mechanical repair, cleaning, detailing, or third-party vendor charges. Those remain separately defined where applicable.

## Shared exclusions
Unless separately contracted, MMC packages do not include:

- Captain or crew services.
- Yacht management or charter management.
- Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, or structural repairs.
- Licensed trade work.
- Diving, underwater inspection, haul-out work, or specialist marine services.
- Routine cleaning/detailing labor.
- Fuel, parts, marina fees, vendor invoices, or other third-party costs.
- Unlimited emergency attendance.
- Legal, insurance, surveying, valuation, or professional engineering advice.

## Commercial rule
The package price covers MMC's defined oversight and coordination service. Third-party costs and materially out-of-scope requests require owner approval before commitment unless an explicitly documented emergency authority applies.
