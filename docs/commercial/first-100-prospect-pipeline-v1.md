# Meridian Marine Co. — First 100 Prospect Pipeline v1

## Objective

Create a disciplined initial prospect universe rather than relying on random outreach.

## Pipeline structure

### Tier 1 — Warm

Existing relationships, referrals, known owners, brokers, marina/service contacts, and trusted professional introductions.

### Tier 2 — Strategic

Targeted yacht owners and marine professionals with strong service-fit characteristics.

### Tier 3 — Network

Broader owner communities, events, and qualified contacts requiring longer-term nurturing.

## Prospect record

Capture:

- Prospect name
- Contact method
- Vessel
- Vessel location
- Relationship/source
- Estimated service fit
- Main pain point
- Decision-maker status
- Last contact
- Next follow-up
- Funnel stage
- Outcome

## First 100 target

Build toward:

- 25 warm prospects
- 35 strategic prospects
- 40 network prospects

The exact names should come from real market research and relationship development rather than fabricated leads.

## Weekly rhythm

Each week:

**ADD → QUALIFY → CONTACT → DISCOVER → PROPOSE → FOLLOW UP → LEARN**

## Learning loop

Track why prospects buy, delay, decline, or choose another solution. Feed those patterns back into package positioning, messaging, pricing, and service design.
