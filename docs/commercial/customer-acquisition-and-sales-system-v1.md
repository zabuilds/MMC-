# Meridian Marine Co. — Customer Acquisition & Sales System v1

## Objective

Build a repeatable path from qualified prospect to active vessel under Harbour, Steward, or Reserve.

## Ideal early customer

Prioritize vessel owners who:

- Own a vessel in Cayman or regularly keep/use one in Cayman
- Spend meaningful time away from the vessel
- Value independent oversight
- Want reliable visibility without managing vendors themselves
- Are willing to pay for recurring peace of mind

## Priority acquisition channels

1. Personal/referral network
2. Yacht brokers and marine professionals
3. Marinas and yacht-service relationships
4. Private client/concierge relationships
5. Direct targeted outreach
6. Owner communities and events

## Sales funnel

**PROSPECT → QUALIFIED → DISCOVERY → SERVICE FIT → PROPOSAL → DECISION → ONBOARDING → ACTIVE VESSEL**

## Qualification

Confirm:

- Vessel location
- Vessel type/complexity
- Owner availability
- Current oversight arrangement
- Main pain points
- Desired visit frequency
- Vendor/maintenance coordination needs
- Readiness expectations
- Decision-maker
- Appropriate package

## Discovery principle

Do not sell inspections. Sell confidence, visibility, and an independent person watching the vessel when the owner is not there.

## Package fit

### Harbour — CI$225/month

Entry recurring oversight for owners who need dependable periodic visibility.

### Steward — CI$395/month

Primary/hero recurring service for owners who want stronger oversight and more frequent attention.

### Reserve — CI$675/month

Highest standard recurring oversight for owners requiring the strongest ongoing presence and readiness support.

### Custom

For complex vessels or requirements that fall outside standard package boundaries.

## Proposal standard

A proposal should clearly show:

- Vessel
- Service selected
- Visit frequency
- Included oversight
- Coordination boundaries
- Reporting approach
- Price
- Start date
- Next step

## Follow-up

Every qualified proposal receives a defined follow-up sequence until one of these outcomes occurs:

**WON → NURTURE → LOST → NOT A FIT**

## Sales KPI priorities

Track:

- Qualified prospects
- Discovery conversations
- Proposals issued
- Close rate
- Days from discovery to decision
- New vessels activated
- Recurring revenue added
- Referral source

## First-100 focus

The early objective is not maximum reach. It is learning which customer profile, acquisition channel, and package combination produces the strongest qualified demand and retention.
