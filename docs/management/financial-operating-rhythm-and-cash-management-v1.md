# Meridian Marine Co. — Financial Operating Rhythm & Cash Management v1

## Objective

Create a simple financial discipline that keeps MMC aware of revenue, cash, costs, profitability, and upcoming commitments while the company is small.

## Weekly financial control

Review:

- Cash available
- Expected customer receipts
- Outstanding invoices
- Upcoming operating expenses
- Material vendor/contractor costs
- Unusual or unexpected spending

## Monthly close

At month-end record:

- Recurring revenue
- New recurring revenue
- Churned/downgraded revenue
- Revenue by package
- Direct service costs
- Operating expenses
- Contribution
- Contribution margin
- Net operating result
- Cash movement

## Cash discipline

Separate three concepts:

**REVENUE ≠ CASH ≠ PROFIT**

A profitable month can still create cash pressure if receipts are delayed or large expenses arrive early.

## Expense categories

Track at minimum:

- Field/service expenses
- Travel/transport
- Equipment and supplies
- Software/platforms
- Communications
- Marketing/sales
- Professional services
- Insurance/administrative costs when applicable
- Contractor/vendor costs
- Other operating expenses

## Spending rules

Before material discretionary spending, ask:

1. Does this directly improve revenue, service quality, risk control, or operating capacity?
2. Is it needed now?
3. Can the same outcome be achieved more simply?
4. Is the expense recurring?
5. What evidence supports the purchase?

## Growth spending

Increase spending in stages as recurring revenue and operating evidence justify it.

Do not build fixed overhead faster than recurring revenue can support.

## Financial warning signals

Escalate review when:

- Cash runway is shrinking materially
- Direct service costs rise faster than revenue
- Contribution margin deteriorates
- Receivables age unexpectedly
- Discretionary spending rises without measurable benefit
- Customer concentration becomes excessive

## Monthly management decision

End each month with:

**PROTECT CASH → PROTECT SERVICE → PROTECT MARGIN → FUND PROVEN GROWTH**

## Principle

MMC should grow from recurring customer value and controlled cash generation, not from premature fixed-cost expansion.
