# Meridian Marine Co. — KPI Framework v1

## Purpose

Measure whether MMC is delivering reliable service, building a healthy recurring client base, and generating sustainable economics.

## Client KPIs

- Active recurring clients
- New clients this period
- Client retention
- Package mix: Harbour / Steward / Reserve / Private
- Client referrals
- Owner satisfaction/feedback

## Operations KPIs

- Scheduled visits
- Completed visits
- On-time visit rate
- Reports delivered on time
- Open actions
- Overdue actions
- Critical/Urgent incidents
- Average coordination workload
- Unplanned work
- Service capacity utilization

## Sales KPIs

- Qualified prospects
- Discovery consultations
- Proposals issued
- New clients won
- Conversion rate
- Acquisition source
- Referral conversion
- Average sales cycle

## Financial KPIs

- Monthly recurring revenue
- Revenue by package
- Average recurring revenue per client
- Third-party pass-through volume
- Gross contribution by package
- Unplanned/non-recurring revenue
- Discounts/credits
- Outstanding receivables

## Quality guardrails

Growth should not be judged independently of:

- On-time service
- Report quality
- Critical response performance
- Client retention
- Open-action backlog
- Owner satisfaction

## Review cadence

Daily: operational exceptions.

Weekly: service delivery, open actions, sales pipeline, capacity.

Monthly: client base, package economics, retention, profitability indicators, and growth decisions.
