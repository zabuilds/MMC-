# Meridian Marine Co. — Weekly Business Review v1

## Purpose

Give management one repeatable weekly review of service delivery, client health, sales, capacity, and exceptions.

## 1. Service delivery

- Visits scheduled vs completed
- Missed/rescheduled visits
- Reports pending
- Critical/Urgent matters
- Open/overdue actions

## 2. Client health

- New clients
- At-risk clients
- Owner concerns/feedback
- Package changes
- Upcoming arrivals/departures

## 3. Sales

- Qualified prospects
- Consultations
- Proposals
- Wins/losses
- Referral activity
- Pipeline value

## 4. Capacity

- Field workload
- Reporting workload
- Coordination workload
- Travel burden
- Upcoming pressure points
- Staffing constraints

## 5. Financial snapshot

- Recurring revenue
- New recurring revenue
- Outstanding receivables
- Unusual expenses
- Third-party pass-through activity

## 6. Incidents and quality

- New incidents
- Resolved incidents
- Service-quality exceptions
- Repeat operational failures

## 7. Decisions

End every weekly review with a short list of decisions, owners, and deadlines.
