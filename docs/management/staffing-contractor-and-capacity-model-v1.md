# Meridian Marine Co. — Staffing, Contractor & Capacity Model v1

## Objective

Scale MMC's people capacity without allowing growth to outrun service quality, cash flow, or management control.

## Founder role

The founder should progressively concentrate on:

- Client trust
- Sales
- Quality assurance
- Strategic relationships
- Exception handling
- System improvement
- Financial control

## Delegation principle

Delegate repeatable work before delegating judgment-heavy work.

### Good early delegation candidates

- Routine administrative preparation
- Scheduling support
- Report formatting/assembly
- Evidence organization
- Basic data entry
- Routine vendor follow-up under defined instructions

### Higher-control responsibilities

Retain tighter oversight over:

- Client relationship decisions
- Critical/Urgent escalation
- Scope interpretation
- Quality approval
- Sensitive communications
- Final report QA
- Vendor disputes
- Material financial commitments

## Operator standard

Anyone performing field service should be evaluated on:

- Reliability
- Observation discipline
- Evidence quality
- Communication
- SOP adherence
- Professional conduct
- Escalation judgment
- Confidentiality

## Capacity model

Monitor workload using:

**ACTIVE VESSELS → VISITS → VISIT HOURS → REPORT HOURS → COORDINATION HOURS → TOTAL CAPACITY**

Do not measure capacity only by vessel count. Vessel complexity and service intensity vary.

## Capacity thresholds

### Green

Capacity is comfortable; service standards are consistently met.

### Yellow

Utilization is high or backlog is increasing; prepare additional capacity.

### Red

Quality, response time, or client experience is deteriorating; stop adding volume until capacity is corrected.

## Hiring/contractor trigger

Consider adding capacity when:

- Demand is sustained rather than temporary
- Existing capacity is consistently high
- Service quality remains strong
- Recurring revenue supports the added cost
- Work can be clearly defined and measured
- The role removes a genuine bottleneck

## Contractor vs employee decision

The business should evaluate classification, control, economics, availability, continuity, and legal requirements before selecting an arrangement. No assumption about legal classification is made by this operating model.

## Onboarding

Every operator receives:

- Role expectations
- Relevant SOPs
- Safety expectations
- Evidence standards
- Communication standards
- Escalation rules
- Confidentiality requirements
- QA expectations

## Performance review

Review:

- Completed work
- QA defects
- Client feedback
- Missed steps
- Reliability
- Response performance
- Training needs

## Capacity expansion sequence

**FOUNDER-LED → SUPPORTED → DELEGATED → SUPERVISED TEAM → OPERATING TEAM**

Expansion should occur only when the operating controls are mature enough to support it.

## Principle

MMC should add people to remove proven bottlenecks, not simply because growth feels exciting. Every additional operator must strengthen—not dilute—the service promise.
