# Meridian Marine Co. — Founder Command Center & Weekly Management System v1

## Objective

Give the founder a simple operating view of MMC so weekly decisions are based on facts rather than noise.

## Command-center categories

Review five areas every week:

1. **SALES** — pipeline and new recurring revenue
2. **CLIENTS** — health, retention, issues, referrals
3. **OPERATIONS** — visits, reports, actions, incidents, vendors
4. **CAPACITY** — workload, operator capacity, founder load
5. **CASH** — revenue, collections, direct costs, upcoming obligations

## Weekly scorecard

Track at minimum:

- Active vessels
- New prospects
- Qualified prospects
- Proposals outstanding
- New recurring revenue
- Visits due/completed
- Reports due/delivered
- Open/overdue actions
- Critical/urgent incidents
- QA defects
- Client health exceptions
- Vendor exceptions
- Capacity utilization
- Founder workload
- Cash collected
- Expected near-term cash

## Monday — Command review

### 1. Sales

- What opportunities can realistically close?
- What follow-ups are due?
- Which acquisition source is producing quality?

### 2. Operations

- What visits are due?
- What reports/actions are outstanding?
- Are any critical or urgent matters open?

### 3. Capacity

- Is the current workload Green, Yellow, or Red?
- What is consuming disproportionate founder time?

### 4. Cash

- What was collected?
- What is due?
- What costs are coming?

### 5. Decisions

Select the small number of decisions that actually matter this week.

## Midweek — Exception review

Focus only on things that are not proceeding normally:

- Client concerns
- Vendor failures
- Late work
- Critical/Urgent issues
- Capacity pressure
- Cash problems

## Friday — Close the loop

Review:

**WHAT HAPPENED → WHAT WAS LEARNED → WHAT REMAINS OPEN → WHAT CHANGES NEXT WEEK**

## Monthly management review

Review trends rather than isolated events:

- Recurring revenue
- Retention/churn
- Pipeline conversion
- Package contribution
- Capacity
- QA
- Vendor performance
- Client health
- Founder dependency
- Cash position

## Decision rules

### Growth

Increase sales activity when service capacity can support it.

### Capacity

Add capacity before service quality breaks.

### Pricing

Review package economics using actual workload and contribution data.

### Quality

Correct systemic defects before adding substantial volume.

### Clients

Prioritize actions that protect trust and recurring relationships.

### Cash

Protect liquidity before discretionary expansion.

## Founder time allocation

As MMC grows, founder time should progressively move toward:

**SALES + CLIENT TRUST + QA + EXCEPTIONS + STRATEGY**

and away from routine administrative work that can be safely delegated.

## Command-center principle

The founder should be able to answer five questions at any point:

**Are we selling? Are we delivering? Are clients happy? Do we have capacity? Do we have cash?**

If any answer is unclear, that uncertainty becomes a management priority.
