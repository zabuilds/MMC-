# Meridian Marine Co. — Management Dashboard & KPI System v1

## Objective

Give management a compact view of whether MMC is growing, delivering, and remaining economically healthy.

## Weekly owner dashboard

### Growth

- Active vessels
- New qualified prospects
- Discovery conversations
- Proposals issued
- New vessels won
- Referral/partner-sourced opportunities

### Service

- Visits due
- Visits completed
- Report turnaround
- Open findings
- Critical/Urgent findings
- Overdue actions
- Vendor exceptions

### Customer

- Client issues
- At-risk relationships
- Retention/churn signals
- Referral opportunities

### Capacity

- Operator workload
- Capacity utilization
- Visit backlog
- Reporting backlog
- Coordination burden

## Monthly business dashboard

### Financial

- Monthly recurring revenue
- New recurring revenue
- Revenue by package
- Revenue by source
- Direct service cost
- Contribution
- Contribution margin
- Operating expenses
- Cash position

### Commercial

- Prospect-to-client conversion
- Discovery-to-proposal conversion
- Proposal-to-close conversion
- Average time to close
- Customer acquisition source
- Average revenue per active vessel

### Operations

- Visit completion rate
- Report turnaround performance
- Finding resolution time
- Critical/Urgent response performance
- Vendor completion/verification performance
- Exception rate

### Customer health

- Retention
- Churn
- Client confidence/satisfaction signals
- Referral rate
- Referral conversion

## KPI hierarchy

### North-star operating outcome

**Active recurring vessels receiving the promised service reliably.**

### Primary business indicators

**MRR + RETENTION + CONTRIBUTION + SERVICE QUALITY**

### Guardrails

**REPORT DELAYS + OVERDUE ACTIONS + CRITICAL/URGENT RESPONSE + CAPACITY OVERLOAD + CLIENT COMPLAINTS**

## Decision rules

If revenue rises while service quality falls, slow growth.

If utilization rises while margins remain healthy and service standards hold, consider adding capacity.

If churn rises, prioritize root-cause correction before increasing acquisition spend.

If package economics weaken, investigate actual service burden before changing pricing.

## Management rhythm

**WEEKLY:** operational control and immediate decisions.

**MONTHLY:** business health, economics, customer health, and capacity.

**QUARTERLY:** strategic direction, pricing, positioning, staffing, partnerships, and growth stage.

## Principle

A dashboard is useful only when it changes decisions. Track fewer meaningful KPIs and act on them consistently.
